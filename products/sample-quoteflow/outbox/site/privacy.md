# Privacy policy

Last updated: [date]

## Who we are
Quoteflow is a web app for freelancers and small agencies. It turns a client brief into a priced proposal with scope, timeline, price options, e-signature and a deposit request in one link.
Company: [legal entity name, country] (open: L4). Postal address: [address] (open: L4). Contact for privacy questions: [email].

## Two roles
- **Your account data.** For the people who sign up to Quoteflow (our users), we decide why and how the data is used. We act as the controller.
- **Your clients' data.** Users put their own clients' details into proposals (for example names, email addresses and signatures). For that data we act on the user's behalf, as a processor. A data processing agreement for users is planned [lawyer to confirm].

## Data we handle
- Account details: name, email address, plan.
- Proposal content: client briefs, scope, timeline, prices, and the names and email addresses of the user's clients.
- Signing data: what the product records when a client signs (for example name or signature, time, other technical data). [Owner to list what is recorded today (L5).]
- Deposit requests: the amount shown on a proposal and the payment link or bank details the user enters. [Pending L2. Draft assumes option A, request only: Quoteflow does not take or hold the money and is not a party to the payment between a user and their client. If a payment processor is added, this section changes.]
- Subscription billing for Pro and Team: [processor and what it receives, pending L3].
- Usage and device data, and cookies: [pending: analytics tool, cookie list and cookie notice. No analytics account exists yet.]

## Why we use it
- To provide the service: create proposals, send them, let clients view and sign them.
- To run accounts, billing and support.
- To keep the service secure.
- To send service emails and, only where allowed, marketing emails with an unsubscribe link [consent wording pending L1 and L4].
