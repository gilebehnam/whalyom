## Executive summary: top 3 priorities
1. "Alternative" pages for Bonsai, HoneyBook, Proposify, PandaDoc (bottom of funnel, specific, winnable long-tail).
2. "Best proposal software for freelancers" plus niche variants (design, developers, marketing, small agency).
3. Free proposal generator / niche proposal templates with pricing options (fits free mode, feeds trial signups). Head term "proposal template" is NOT a target yet (very competitive, H).

## Quick wins for a new site (ranked)
1. Alternative pages with a persona suffix ("honeybook alternative for developers", "proposify alternative for small agencies").
2. Region variants (UK/AU/CA) once C14 is verified; otherwise skip the currency/tax angle.
3. "Proposal and deposit request" and "brief to proposal" feature terms (low competition, direct match to C2, C5, C6).
4. Good-better-best and expiry-date guides (specific, low competition).
5. Free proposal generator as the link-earning asset.

## Do not target yet
- Head terms: "proposal template", "proposal software", "contract template", "invoice". High difficulty, mixed intent.
- Sales-team terms (quote software, CPQ): outside ICP (PandaDoc territory).
- Event/wedding proposal terms: outside ICP (HoneyBook core).
- EU terms: market unconfirmed (C13).
