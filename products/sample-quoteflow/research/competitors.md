## Competitor facts (own pages, checked 2026-11-03, USD, US version)
Prices, trial lengths, card requirements and their status (verified or conflict) are in **research/pricing-intel.md, section 2 (the single price table)**. Read that table before using any competitor number.

### Bonsai (https://www.hellobonsai.com/pricing)
- Plans: Basic, Essentials, Premium, Elite (per user; Elite needs 3 users minimum).
- Proposals and contracts start at Essentials. Basic has no proposals.
- Essentials adds invoices and payments. Premium adds project insights, pipeline, custom fields, removal of Bonsai branding, and integrations (QuickBooks, Xero, Zapier, Calendly, Google). Elite adds custom permissions.
- Online payments come with invoices on Essentials and above. Processing fees vary by method (fee table not captured).
- E-signature: not listed as a plan feature on the pricing page (it lists "Proposals & Contracts"). Do not say either way until the proposal-software page is checked.
- Third-party review sites quote older tiers (Starter, Professional, Business). Treat them as stale.

### HoneyBook (https://www.honeybook.com/pricing)
- Plans: Starter, Essentials, Premium. Pricing and trial: see pricing-intel.md.
- Starter: unlimited clients and projects, invoices and payments, proposals and contracts, client portal, up to 2 lead forms, HoneyBook AI. Essentials adds scheduler, automations, QuickBooks Online, up to 2 team members, SMS reminders, removes "Powered by HoneyBook". Premium adds unlimited team members, priority support, multiple companies, advanced reports.
- The page describes proposals as letting clients book, sign and pay from one file. E-signature is on contracts and forms.
- Payments: the FAQ and the plan table disagree on card fees. The figures are in pricing-intel.md, section 2d. Do not quote card fees.
- 60-day money-back guarantee (from the T3 read; not re-checked by K1).
- Markets and currencies: US (USD), Canada (CAD), UK (GBP), Australia (AUD) in the country selector. Prices shown are USD only. So the "UK/AU gap against HoneyBook" hypothesis in profile.md is FALSE. Drop it.

### Proposify (https://www.proposify.com/pricing)
- Plans on the pricing section: Build, Grow, Scale. Pricing and trial: see pricing-intel.md. Two rows are in conflict (Build annual, Grow annual).
- The send-limit table uses different plan names (Basic, Team, Business, Enterprise). Do not map them to Build, Grow and Scale without a hand re-read.
- E-signature included on all plans. Payments via Stripe (per-plan detail not given).
- Trial: 14 days on Build, no card (per the page). Grow and Scale do not list a trial.
- Custom template design fee on Build: see pricing-intel.md, section 2d (from the T3 read; not re-checked by K1).

### PandaDoc (https://www.pandadoc.com/pricing/)
- Plans: Free, Starter, Business, Enterprise (custom, "Let's talk"). Pricing and trial: see pricing-intel.md. The Business trial is in conflict.
- Free: 5 documents per month (shared), e-sign, editor, tracking, limited API.
- Starter: unlimited uploads and e-signatures, templates up to 5. Business: custom quotes, CRM integrations, branding, approvals, bulk send, payments, templates unlimited.
- Payment collection is listed on Business and Enterprise only, not Free or Starter (from the T3 read).
- The page makes an annual-billing savings claim (figure in pricing-intel.md, section 2d).

## What this changes for Quoteflow copy and claims
- C16 ("cheaper than ...") stays needs-proof and is not supported on a like-for-like basis. Pro's price is level with PandaDoc Starter's per-seat annual price, and PandaDoc Starter has no payment collection. Bonsai Essentials is level with Pro on annual billing, but it is higher on monthly billing and includes more (invoices, time tracking). Safe comparison framing is scope (one narrow proposal job) and plan structure, not "cheaper".
- Possible accurate contrasts, each citing the competitor URL and date: PandaDoc Free caps at 5 documents per month against Quoteflow Free at 3 proposals per month (do not frame as a win). HoneyBook has no free plan and its entry tier is billed yearly. Proposify has no free plan and is priced per user. Bonsai proposals need the Essentials tier or above.
- No competitor has a free plan except PandaDoc (document cap, not proposal-specific). Quoteflow Free (3 proposals per month) is a legitimate difference; the claim is C7.
- Team angle: Quoteflow Team (C9, 5 seats) against per-seat plans at Bonsai, PandaDoc and Proposify. The per-seat contrast is accurate for the rows marked verified in pricing-intel.md. The Proposify Build annual cell is in conflict and must not be quoted. Compare on features too; Quoteflow lacks approvals, integrations and CRM (C17).
- HoneyBook and Bonsai both lead with suite functions (invoicing, CRM, scheduling). The "narrow proposal tool" frame holds.
