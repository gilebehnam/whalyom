## 4. PR-FAQ spine (launch-day tense, internal draft)

**Headline:** Quoteflow lets freelancers and small agencies send a priced proposal, collect a signature and request a deposit from one link.

**Lead:** Today Quoteflow opens to freelance designers, developers and marketers, and to agencies of 2 to 10 people. A client brief becomes a proposal with scope, timeline and price options. The client signs and gets the deposit request in the same link. A free plan covers 3 proposals a month. Pro is $19 a month and Team is $49 a month for 5 seats. Every Pro trial lasts 14 days.

**Problem paragraph (theme, not a quote):** Freelancers say they redo scope and price for each brief and send the signature and deposit request through other tools. [paraphrased second-hand theme, voc.md]

**Customer quote:** none. No customers exist (C18). Do not invent one.

**Empty-chair test:** every sentence above is something Solo Sam would care about (price, free plan, one link). The agency line serves Alex. Nothing about speed or ranking survives.

### External FAQ
1. **What does it do?** Turns a brief into a priced proposal with scope, timeline, price options, e-signature and a deposit request in one link (C2-C6).
2. **What does it cost?** Free: 3 proposals a month. Pro $19/month. Team $49/month for 5 seats. 14-day Pro trial (C7-C10). Open: what Pro adds over Free, extra-seat price, annual pricing.
3. **Do you process the deposit?** Say only "deposit request". Owner must answer (C15).
4. **Does it connect to my tools?** No. There are no integrations (C17). Say so plainly.
5. **How is it different from Bonsai, HoneyBook, Proposify, PandaDoc?** Answer on fit only: Quoteflow does the proposal job and nothing else. No named comparison until their pages are checked and dated (C16).

### Internal FAQ
1. Do we have any proof of speed? No. C12 needs a timed demo.
2. Is the category crowded? Yes. Other "brief to proposal" tools exist and the four known competitors rate well. We compete on fit.
3. What happens on a UK, CA or AU buyer's first invoice? Unknown: currency and VAT/GST (C14).
4. Is the EU in or out? product.json says in, brief says out. Treat as out until the owner answers (C13).
5. What if no one signs up? The goal is 50 trial signups in 30 days at a $0 cap. Reach depends on free channels, and there is no domain yet.
