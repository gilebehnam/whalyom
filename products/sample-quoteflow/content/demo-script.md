## 1. What this video is for

1. **Evidence for C12.** C12 ("creates a proposal in minutes") is `needs-proof`. A continuous, unedited run with a visible stopwatch is the proof. The measured time on the stopwatch decides what we may say. Nothing in the narration says "in minutes" or any duration.

3. Lead message is Idea A: brief to a signed proposal and a deposit request, in one link.

Format: screen recording, 16:9, 1920x1080, founder voiceover (or captions only). Target length 75 seconds, hard limits 60 to 90 seconds.

## 3. The stopwatch rule (this is the proof)

- A stopwatch overlay sits in the top-right corner for the whole video, from 0:00.0 to the final frame. Large, high contrast, one decimal place.
- Start the stopwatch **at the moment the brief is pasted** (the clock starts when the user's work starts, not when the app is open). Stop it at the last screen action, which is the deposit request showing as sent in the client view.
- Record in **one take with no cuts** in the app portion. A jump cut makes the time worthless as C12 evidence. Edits are allowed only before the clock starts (intro) and after it stops (end card).
- Use a second, separate clock for the full video length (shown in the editor, not on screen).
- The time on the overlay is a fact about this one run. Do 3 timed runs off camera first and keep all 3 times in the evidence log (section 7).

Overlay tools (any one): OBS clock source, Screen Studio / Camtasia timer callout, or a free browser stopwatch placed in the recording frame. Do not fake or add the overlay in post-production over a cut recording.

## 4. Pre-recording setup

- Clean browser profile, no personal tabs or notifications. Zoom to 125% so the text reads on a phone.
- Use a **fictional client and a fictional brief**. Do not use a real client or a real company logo. Make it plain, e.g. "Brightside Dental" (invented) wants a 5-page website refresh. Check the name matches no real business.
- Prepare the brief as text in the clipboard (about 80 to 120 words: goal, pages, deadline, budget range).
- Account on the Pro trial or Free plan, logged in, empty dashboard, so the first proposal is the one on screen.
- Price options prepared as the product defaults, or typed live. Whatever happens live must be done within the stopwatch time.
- A second browser window (or private window) logged out, to act as the client for the signing and deposit beats.
- Sample signature typed or drawn by the owner in the client window, fictional name.
- The deposit beat shows a **request** (amount and a "Request deposit" action or equivalent). Do not show a bank, card or payment confirmation screen unless the real app processes payments. Owner confirms how deposits work (C15) before this beat is recorded.

## 8. Production notes

- Recording tool: OBS (free) or Loom free tier. Screen Studio if on Mac, for cursor highlight and auto-zoom. 1080p minimum, 30 fps.
- Zoom moments (add in post, as zoom of the same continuous recording, not a cut): shot 3 scope list, shot 4 price options, shot 8 deposit request.
- Audio: external microphone, quiet room, record voiceover separately in a second pass if live narration slows the clicks (the stopwatch time must come from the silent live run, so record that first and narrate over it).
