## 1. Category
Proposal software for freelancers and small agencies. It is a narrow proposal tool (brief to signed proposal plus deposit request), not an all-in-one freelancer suite and not general document automation.
- Category frame to test: "brief-to-proposal app". Hypothesis from research/competitors.md, not yet validated.

## 2. Problem
Freelancers and small agencies redo scope, timeline and pricing for every client brief. They then send the proposal, the contract or signature, and the deposit request through separate tools or emails.
- VoC themes (paraphrased, second-hand, not quotable; see research/voc.md): proposals take too long; vague scope leads to unpaid extra work; people pad prices because they underestimate; proposals without an expiry get forgotten.

## 4. Value proposition (6-part JTBD)

**For freelancers and 2-10 person agencies**
1. **Who:** Freelance designers, developers, marketers and small agencies in the US, UK, CA and AU who reply to client briefs with proposals.
2. **Why (job):** Turn a client brief into a proposal the client can accept: clear scope, a timeline and a price, then get it signed and get a deposit.
3. **What before:** Docs, Notion or Canva templates, or email plus a PDF. The scope and price get rewritten each time. Signing (DocuSign and similar) and deposits (invoicing tools) happen in separate steps. Suite tools (Bonsai, HoneyBook) do more than a proposal needs.
4. **How (verified features):** The brief becomes a priced proposal with scope, timeline, price options, e-signature and a deposit request, all in one link.
5. **What after:** One link takes the client from proposal to signature to deposit. The client picks from the price options you set.
6. **Alternatives:** Bonsai, HoneyBook, Proposify, PandaDoc, templates, e-sign-only tools, invoicing tools, and AI chat used ad hoc. The difference: one narrow job done in one link, and a free plan plus low entry pricing. Competitor details are UNVERIFIED (research/competitors.md).

**Statement (internal, uses verified facts only):**
For freelancers and small agencies who answer client briefs, Quoteflow is a proposal app that turns a brief into a priced proposal with scope, timeline, price options, e-signature and a deposit request in one link. Unlike all-in-one freelancer suites, it does that one job.

Do not use in copy yet: "in minutes" (speed claim, needs proof, C12). Any comparison with a named competitor also has to wait until that competitor's pages are checked.
