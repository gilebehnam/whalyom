## Conversion, UX, speed and accessibility

- **European Accessibility Act scope per Your Europe (checked 12 Jul 2026): requirements apply to products and services placed on the market after 28 Jun 2025, including online shops, ticketing platforms and banking services. Service businesses with fewer than 10 employees and under EUR 2 million turnover are exempt; self-service terminals in use before 28 Jun 2025 may continue up to 20 years. An Interim Final Rule published 20 Apr 2026 extended compliance by about a year: 26 Apr 2027 for entities with 50,000+ population, 26 Apr 2028 for smaller entities and special districts. ## Pricing and offers

- **UK subscription contracts regime (DMCC Act Part 4 Ch 2): draft government guidance published in Oct 2026 (reported as 19 Oct 2026) says the rules apply to subscription contracts entered into on or after 1 January 2027, but the accompanying draft Consumer Subscription Contracts (Cancellation and Information) Regulations 2026 still need Parliament's approval, so the date is not yet confirmed. Under the regime traders must give key pre-contract information, send reminder notices before renewals and after free or discounted trials, allow a 14-day cooling-off period, and let online sign-ups end the contract online in a straightforward way.** (2026-10-19; official, medium) [source](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6acf97da2de3184ccf8b814e/subscription-contracts-rules-guidance.pdf) `K-d61ac4930b`
- **UK DMCC Act unfair commercial practices rules apply from April 2025 (6 Apr 2025): drip pricing is banned and invitations to purchase must show the total price including mandatory fees. On 15 Apr 2026 the CMA fined the AA GBP 4.2 million (after a 40% settlement discount from GBP 7 million) for a mandatory GBP 3 booking fee left out of the upfront price; fines can reach 10% of global turnover (or GBP 300,000 if higher). CMA guidance on pricing (CMA209) covers drip and partitioned pricing.** (2026-04-15; official, medium) [source](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/cma-orders-the-aa-and-bsm-driving-schools-to-refund-learner-drivers-over-drip-pricing) `K-4d4203632e`
- **FTC click-to-cancel status: the Eighth Circuit vacated the FTC's 2024 Negative Option Rule amendments on 8 Jul 2025 (Custom Communications v. FTC). On 11 Mar 2026 the FTC announced an advance notice of proposed rulemaking on negative option marketing (published in the Federal Register on 13 Mar 2026), asking whether to amend the rule and referring to the vacated 2024 rule as one option. No federal click-to-cancel rule is in force; ROSCA, the FTC Act and state laws still apply.** (2026-03-11; official, medium) [source](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/03/ftc-seeks-public-comment-response-advance-notice-proposed-rulemaking-regarding-negative-option) `K-edadbf9c49`
