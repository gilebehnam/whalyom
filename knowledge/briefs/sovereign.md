## Privacy, consent and data law

- **Connecticut Data Privacy Act (AG guidance): it applies to controllers processing data of 35,000+ consumers, to anyone processing sensitive data or selling personal data, and to all consumer health data controllers. Since 1 Jan 2025, every covered controller must honor opt-out preference signals such as Global Privacy Control for targeted ads and sales. Sensitive data needs consent. Selling data of, or targeting ads to, consumers under 16 needs opt-in consent, and online services may not target ads to known minors under 18. Profiling needs an impact assessment.** (2026-11-03; official, medium) [source](https://portal.ct.gov/ag/sections/privacy/the-connecticut-data-privacy-act) `K-db9a4b5319`

## Digital marketing strategy and management consulting

- **Incrementality testing in Google Ads (Conversion Lift): a test splits the audience into a treatment group that sees ads and a control group that does not, by user (user-based) or by geography (geo-based, which supports offline data); lift is the difference in conversions. User-based studies report incremental conversions, relative lift, iCPA and iROAS (iROAS only if conversion values are assigned); geo-based studies report iROAS, incremental conversions, conversion value and cost. Not available to every account (contact your Google account representative). Use to validate platform-reported ROAS before scaling a channel.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/12003020?hl=en) `K-d9b13f1598`

## AI agents, models and agentic commerce

- **OWASP LLM01:2025 Prompt Injection: direct injection comes from user input; indirect injection comes from external content (websites, files) that the model processes. OWASP says no foolproof prevention is known, only layered mitigation: constrain the model's role, validate output formats with deterministic code, filter inputs and outputs, enforce least privilege, require human approval for high-risk actions, segregate and mark untrusted external content, and run regular adversarial testing.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm01-prompt-injection/) `K-0a3bd69f71`
- **YouTube requires creators to set 'AI use' at upload for realistic AI-generated or meaningfully altered content: a real person saying or doing what they didn't, altered footage of real events or places, realistic scenes that never happened, or AI music as the main focus. Not needed for clearly unrealistic or animated content, minor edits, or AI used only for scripts, titles, thumbnails or ideas. YouTube may auto-label via its own tools or C2PA metadata. Disclosure alone does not limit reach or monetization; repeated non-disclosure can mean removal or YPP suspension.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en) `K-43608d5348`

Advice: share product, price and stock data with agents but keep customer identity and loyalty history in-house, and keep exclusive experiences on owned channels. Use: design loyalty perks agents can compare, plus owned-channel extras.** (2026-10-21; primary, high) [source](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2026/retailers-win-ai-shopping-agents) `K-e9330284c3`
