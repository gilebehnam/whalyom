## Analytics, tracking and measurement

- **Consent mode implementation: call gtag('consent','default',...) on every page before any config or event command, since out-of-order code breaks defaults. Use wait_for_update (e.g. 500 ms) when the CMP loads asynchronously; set region-specific defaults with ISO 3166-2 codes (US-CA overrides US; a default without region covers everyone else); send gtag('consent','update',...) as soon as the user chooses and persist the choice in a first-party cookie.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://developers.google.com/tag-platform/security/guides/consent) `K-0f5c3b780a`
- **GA4 standard property configuration limits: 50 event-scoped custom dimensions, 25 user-scoped custom dimensions, 50 event-scoped custom metrics, 30 key events, 100 audiences, 50 custom insights, 200 explorations per user (500 shared), 400 Ads links, and 10M events per exploration query. Analytics blocks new items once a limit is reached, so budget custom dimensions in the tracking plan and archive unused ones.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/12229528?hl=en) `K-1317b85450`
- **Basic vs advanced consent mode: basic blocks Google tags until the user interacts with the banner and sends nothing (not even consent state) if consent is denied, so Ads conversion modelling uses a general model. Advanced loads tags with consent defaulted to denied and sends cookieless pings, enabling an advertiser-specific model once a data threshold is met. Choose per the company's legal position; advanced recovers more modelled conversions.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://developers.google.com/tag-platform/security/concepts/consent-mode) `K-1aab001f7d`
- **GA4 Measurement Protocol sends server-to-server and offline events over HTTP, but it is meant to augment gtag, Tag Manager or Firebase collection, not replace it; a tagging method must already be in place. Join events to users with client_id (web) or app_instance_id (app), include session_id within 24 hours to attach session geo and device data, and test payloads against the validation server first.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://developers.google.com/analytics/devguides/collection/protocol/ga4) `K-71e291eb75`

## App stores and extension stores

- **Apple custom product pages: up to 70 extra versions of the App Store page per app, each with its own screenshots, app previews and promotional text per localisation. Pages can be assigned keywords so they show in organic search for those terms (each keyword combination unique to one page), can carry a deep link (iOS/iPadOS 18+), are reviewed without an app update, and can be the destination of Apple Ads on Today, Search and results. Build one page per ad angle or audience.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/custom-product-pages/) `K-48468fdabc`

## Commerce feeds, shopping and checkout

- **Organization-level return policy markup: nest MerchantReturnPolicy under Organization or OnlineStore via hasMerchantReturnPolicy on one page (e.g. the returns page), not every page. Required: either applicableCountry (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2, up to 50) plus returnPolicyCategory (with merchantReturnDays for a finite window), or merchantReturnLink. Recommended: returnFees, returnMethod, refundType, restockingFee, and returnPolicySeasonalOverride for holiday windows.** (2026-11-03; official, medium) [source](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/return-policy) `K-0410b3d507`
- **Merchant listing vs product snippet markup: merchant listings require an Offer (product snippets also accept AggregateOffer) because the merchant must be the seller, and they require a price greater than zero. Use product snippet markup for editorial review or comparison pages, and merchant listing markup only on pages where shoppers can buy from you.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/merchant-listing) `K-0a5b0b5299`
- **Merchant Center identifiers: submit gtin whenever the manufacturer assigned one (max 14 digits, no dashes or spaces); identifier_exists defaults to yes and should be set to no only when the product truly lacks identifiers for its category. Setting it to no for a product that has valid identifiers can get the product disapproved. ISBN-10 is deprecated; convert to ISBN-13.** (2026-11-03; official, medium) [source](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/7052112?hl=en) `K-0cb278563c`
- **Google free listings show products at no cost on the Shopping tab, Search (rich results, Popular Products, Shopping Knowledge Panel), Images and Lens, YouTube (limited countries), Maps, Gemini and Business Profile; free local listings need an opt-in. They are usually on by default (Admin access needed to change them) and work alongside paid ads. Check free listings status before spending on Shopping ads for a new store.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/9199328?hl=en) `K-2853dcb89d`
- **Google Merchant Center required product attributes: id (max 50 chars), title (max 150) or structured_title, description (max 5,000) or structured_description, link on the verified domain, image_link, availability (in_stock, out_of_stock, preorder, backorder; availability_date required for preorder), price with ISO 4217 currency, brand for new products (except movies, books, music), mpn only when no GTIN, and condition for used or refurbished items. Apparel and variants also need item_group_id, color, size, gender and age_group in some countries.** (2026-11-03; official, medium) [source](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/7052112?hl=en) `K-2cb2187f7e`
- **Merchant Center energy labels: energy efficiency class attributes now apply only to products targeting Switzerland, Norway or the UK; EU energy label data must be sent through the certification attribute. Smartphones and tablets came under EU energy label rules from 20 Jun 2025, and tumble dryers were rescaled from 1 Jul 2025. Feeds for EU appliance or phone sellers must carry certification data.** (2026-11-03; official, medium) [source](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/7052112?hl=en) `K-4b7f74cba9`

## Video, connected TV and programmatic buying

- **IAB Tech Lab CTV Programmatic Guide (the page shows no date, only a 2026 copyright; the guide is treated as undated, and the 7 Oct 2026 date below belongs to the separate sellers.json page): in CTV, ads.txt and app-ads.txt declare authorised sellers (the INVENTORYPARTNERDOMAIN value lets another domain be named an authorised seller); sellers.json plus the SupplyChain object let a buyer see the parties selling or reselling a bid request. The sellers.json page (last updated 7 Oct 2026) says SSPs and exchanges are expected to post their sellers.json files. For spoofed devices the guide describes manufacturer device attestation. Ask your DSP to report spend by final seller.** (2026-11-04; official, medium) [source](https://iabtechlab.com/standards/advanced-tv/ctv-programmatic-guide/) `K-00f2b63092`
- **IAB Tech Lab CTV Programmatic Guide (undated page, 2026 copyright), buying mechanics: it describes OpenRTB 2.6 podded bidding (pod signals include ad sequence, total pod length and maximum ads per pod); its brand-suitability example tags content with Content Taxonomy 3.1 and has the DSP use the 'bcat' attribute to block ads; and it says an impression is flagged not viewable when the video is obscured, outside the active window or the TV is off (Open Measurement). Ask a CTV DSP whether it supports these before launch.** (2026-11-04; official, medium) [source](https://iabtechlab.com/standards/advanced-tv/ctv-programmatic-guide/) `K-069ab3d6de`
- **Google Ads Help on QR codes for CTV (undated; footer shows only 2026): QR codes are generated automatically on Demand Gen and Performance Max campaigns targeting CTV (skippable in-stream and Shorts), and via a 'show final URL as QR code on TV Screens' checkbox on YouTube Select video campaigns. They are not available on YouTube TV or Google TV inventory, and QR position and timing are fixed. Google advises avoiding embedding QR codes in the video file (a recommendation). Non-skippable ads are not supported in Demand Gen or Performance Max.** (2026-11-04; official, medium) [source](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16903036?hl=en) `K-ac2cb89838`
