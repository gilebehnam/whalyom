## Pricing and offers

- **UK subscription contracts regime (DMCC Act Part 4 Ch 2): draft government guidance published in Oct 2026 (reported as 19 Oct 2026) says the rules apply to subscription contracts entered into on or after 1 January 2027, but the accompanying draft Consumer Subscription Contracts (Cancellation and Information) Regulations 2026 still need Parliament's approval, so the date is not yet confirmed. Under the regime traders must give key pre-contract information, send reminder notices before renewals and after free or discounted trials, allow a 14-day cooling-off period, and let online sign-ups end the contract online in a straightforward way.** (2026-10-19; official, medium) [source](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6acf97da2de3184ccf8b814e/subscription-contracts-rules-guidance.pdf) `K-d61ac4930b`
- **UK DMCC Act unfair commercial practices rules apply from April 2025 (6 Apr 2025): drip pricing is banned and invitations to purchase must show the total price including mandatory fees. On 15 Apr 2026 the CMA fined the AA GBP 4.2 million (after a 40% settlement discount from GBP 7 million) for a mandatory GBP 3 booking fee left out of the upfront price; fines can reach 10% of global turnover (or GBP 300,000 if higher). CMA guidance on pricing (CMA209) covers drip and partitioned pricing.** (2026-04-15; official, medium) [source](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/cma-orders-the-aa-and-bsm-driving-schools-to-refund-learner-drivers-over-drip-pricing) `K-4d4203632e`
- **FTC click-to-cancel status: the Eighth Circuit vacated the FTC's 2024 Negative Option Rule amendments on 8 Jul 2025 (Custom Communications v. FTC). On 11 Mar 2026 the FTC announced an advance notice of proposed rulemaking on negative option marketing (published in the Federal Register on 13 Mar 2026), asking whether to amend the rule and referring to the vacated 2024 rule as one option. No federal click-to-cancel rule is in force; ROSCA, the FTC Act and state laws still apply.** (2026-03-11; official, medium) [source](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/03/ftc-seeks-public-comment-response-advance-notice-proposed-rulemaking-regarding-negative-option) `K-edadbf9c49`
- **FTC v. Amazon (settled 25 Sep 2025): Amazon agreed to a USD 1 billion civil penalty and USD 1.5 billion in consumer redress for about 35 million consumers over unwanted Prime enrollment and hard-to-use cancellation (FTC Act and ROSCA). It must show a clearly visible option to decline Prime, clearly disclose key terms during enrollment, and let people cancel using the same method they used to sign up; a third-party supervisor monitors the refunds.** (2025-09-25; official, high) [source](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/09/ftc-secures-historic-25-billion-settlement-against-amazon) `K-ce55ae0b38`
- **California's amended Automatic Renewal Law (AB 2863), for contracts entered, amended or extended from 1 Jul 2025, requires express affirmative consent to auto-renewal terms, online cancellation for online sign-ups without steps that obstruct or delay it, an annual reminder through the enrollment channel stating product, charge amount and frequency and how to cancel, notice 3-21 days before free or discounted trials over 31 days end, and notice 7-30 days before an accepted price increase.** (2025-09-04; official, high) [source](https://oag.ca.gov/news/press-releases/attorney-general-bonta-issues-consumer-alert-california%E2%80%99s-automatic-renewal-law) `K-04924b11ee`
- **The FTC Rule on Unfair or Deceptive Fees (announced 17 Dec 2024, effective 12 May 2025) covers only live-event tickets and short-term lodging. Covered businesses must clearly show the total price including all mandatory fees whenever they show a price, make that total more prominent than other pricing, and not misrepresent any fee. Taxes and shipping may be excluded but must be disclosed before payment. Other industries face case-by-case FTC Act enforcement, so show all-in prices anyway.** (2024-12-17; official, high) [source](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/12/federal-trade-commission-announces-bipartisan-rule-banning-junk-ticket-hotel-fees) `K-62f88257de`
- **CJEU, Case C-330/23 Aldi Sud (26 Sep 2024): a price reduction announced as a percentage, or as a promotional claim stressing an advantageous price such as 'price highlight', must be calculated on the basis of the lowest price the trader applied in the 30 days before the reduction. Merely showing that lowest price for information is not enough.** (2024-09-26; official, high) [source](https://curia.europa.eu/jcms/upload/docs/application/pdf/2024-09/cp240152en.pdf) `K-391cc374c3`
- **Directive (EU) 2023/2673 adds Article 11a to the Consumer Rights Directive, applied from 19 Jun 2026: for distance contracts concluded online, traders must offer a withdrawal function labeled 'withdraw from contract here' or an unambiguous equivalent, continuously available during the withdrawal period, prominent and easy to reach. It lets the consumer send an online withdrawal statement with name, contract details and the channel for confirmation, which the trader must confirm on a durable medium. Add it to every EU checkout and account area.** (2023-11-28; official, high) [source](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2023/2673/oj/eng) `K-1599e3e75f`
- **EU Price Indication Directive Art. 6a (added by Omnibus Directive 2019/2161, applied from 28 May 2022): any announced price reduction must show the prior price, meaning the lowest price the trader applied in a period of at least 30 days before the reduction. Member States may set different rules for goods that deteriorate or expire quickly, a shorter period for products on sale under 30 days, and, for progressively increased reductions, use the price before the first reduction. Check the national transposition for each market.** (2019-12-18; official, high) [source](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/2161/oj/eng) `K-6bde58c230`
- **Lacetera, Pope and Sydnor (American Economic Review, 2012, 102(5):2206-36), using more than 22 million wholesale used-car sales, found left-digit bias: prices drop sharply when odometer readings cross 10,000-mile thresholds, with smaller drops at 1,000-mile thresholds, driven mainly by final consumers. It is large-scale field evidence that buyers anchor on leftmost digits, which supports keeping displayed prices just under a round threshold.** (2012-08-01; research, high) [source](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/aer.102.5.2206) `K-af4f44fdee`
- **Thomas and Morwitz (Journal of Consumer Research, 2005, 32(1):54-64): nine-ending prices such as 2.99 are perceived as smaller than a price one cent higher only when the leftmost digit changes (2.99 vs 3.00, not 2.89 vs 2.90). The effect is stronger when the comparison price is close and also appears with other multi-digit numbers. Use charm endings where they drop the left digit; elsewhere they add little.** (2005-06-01; research, high) [source](https://academic.oup.com/jcr/article-abstract/32/1/54/1796211) `K-5ac5a89372`
- **Anderson and Simester (Quantitative Marketing and Economics, 2003, 1(1):93-110) ran three field experiments in a mail-order catalog varying price endings: a 9 ending increased demand in all three. The lift was larger for new items than for items sold in earlier years, and there is some evidence that 9 endings work less well when 'Sale' cues are also used. 9 endings likely help most when buyers have little price information, such as new products or launches.** (2003-03-01; research, high) [source](https://ideas.repec.org/a/kap/qmktec/v1y2003i1p93-110.html) `K-77b468a1de`
- **Ariely, Loewenstein and Prelec (Quarterly Journal of Economics, 2003, 118(1):73-106), six experiments: initial valuations of familiar products and experiences were strongly influenced by arbitrary anchors, sometimes the last digits of a person's social security number, while later valuations stayed consistent relative to that first anchor. The first price a buyer sees shapes willingness to pay, so show the reference plan or price first, and keep any anchor truthful under EU and UK price rules.** (2003-02-01; research, high) [source](https://academic.oup.com/qje/article-abstract/118/1/73/1917051) `K-83d61597bb`
