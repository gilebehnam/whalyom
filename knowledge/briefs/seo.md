## AI search and answer engines

- **OpenAI's product feed spec for ChatGPT accepts JSONL, CSV or TSV. Discovery requires item_id, title, description, url, brand, seller_name, image_url, availability and price. is_eligible_search defaults to true, and setting it false also blocks checkout. Checkout needs a separately onboarded integration plus seller_privacy_policy and seller_tos. Ads stay off unless a feed-level default enables them. Update prices and availability when they change; sale or availability dates in the feed don't schedule anything. Spec read 3 Nov 2026.** (2026-11-03; official, medium) [source](https://developers.openai.com/commerce/specs/feed) `K-61bbefed06`
- **Applebot crawls for Spotlight, Siri and Safari search features, and its data may also help train Apple's generative AI models. Applebot-Extended doesn't crawl: it only controls how Apple uses data Applebot has already crawled, so disallow Applebot-Extended in robots.txt to opt out of training. Applebot-Extended rules don't affect search ranking. The nosnippet directive keeps content out of AI-generated answers.** (2026-11-03; official, medium) [source](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119829) `K-a84b169d05`

## Content quality and editorial standards

- **Google lists search-engine-first warning signs: producing many articles on varied topics to chase traffic, summarizing others without adding value, writing about trends without expertise, changing dates to seem fresh, and writing to a word count - Google says it has no preferred word count. Self-check: would you bookmark, share or recommend the page; ask trusted people unaffiliated with your site for honest feedback.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) `K-0ce1dcd8a9`
- **Google's guidance on generative AI content: AI can help research and structure original content, but content must meet Search Essentials and spam policies; generating many pages without adding value may be scaled content abuse. Manually fact-check and review all AI output before publishing, including titles, meta descriptions, structured data and image alt text, because models can be wrong. Consider telling readers how automation was used. Quality rater ratings do not directly influence ranking.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content) `K-13c7cba0b7`
- **Google's 'helpful, people-first content' page asks Who, How and Why. Who: show clear bylines linking to author background; fabricated authors, AI-generated headshots or false credentials are deceptive. How: explain how content was made, including automation or AI, when readers would reasonably wonder. Why: the main purpose must be helping visitors, not attracting search visits. Trust is the most important part of E-E-A-T, and YMYL topics (health, finance, safety) get extra weight.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) `K-289c2d952c`
- **Google Merchant Center requires AI-generated product images to carry IPTC DigitalSourceType metadata, normally TrainedAlgorithmicMedia (also CompositeSynthetic or AlgorithmicMedia), and the tag must not be stripped, including from Product Studio images. AI-written product titles and descriptions go in structured_title and structured_description with digital_source_type 'trained_algorithmic_media' and the text in content, instead of or alongside title and description.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/14743464?hl=en) `K-b3d4f496b7`
- **Google Article structured data has no required properties. Mark up every author shown on the page as a separate author entry, typed Person or Organization, with url or sameAs to a page about them; author.name holds only the name, with job titles, honorifics and publisher in their own properties. Use ISO 8601 datePublished and dateModified with a timezone (otherwise Googlebot's timezone is used). Provide images in 16x9, 4x3 and 1x1 ratios of at least 50,000 pixels (width times height) each.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/article) `K-c01a02696b`

## AI agents, models and agentic commerce

- **OWASP LLM01:2025 Prompt Injection: direct injection comes from user input; indirect injection comes from external content (websites, files) that the model processes. OWASP says no foolproof prevention is known, only layered mitigation: constrain the model's role, validate output formats with deterministic code, filter inputs and outputs, enforce least privilege, require human approval for high-risk actions, segregate and mark untrusted external content, and run regular adversarial testing.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm01-prompt-injection/) `K-0a3bd69f71`
- **YouTube requires creators to set 'AI use' at upload for realistic AI-generated or meaningfully altered content: a real person saying or doing what they didn't, altered footage of real events or places, realistic scenes that never happened, or AI music as the main focus. Not needed for clearly unrealistic or animated content, minor edits, or AI used only for scripts, titles, thumbnails or ideas. YouTube may auto-label via its own tools or C2PA metadata. Disclosure alone does not limit reach or monetization; repeated non-disclosure can mean removal or YPP suspension.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en) `K-43608d5348`
- **Anthropic's Usage Policy effective 12 Nov 2026: consumer-facing chatbots and agents must disclose that users are interacting with AI rather than a human, at the start of each session or in the interface; political content must not be false or misleading, and mass automated messages to voters must not conceal their artificial origin; high-risk AI recommendations (legal, medical, finance, credit, employment, housing) need meaningful review by a qualified person.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/aup) `K-558ee2c6d4`
- **Google SynthID embeds invisible watermarks in AI images and video (designed to survive cropping, filters, frame-rate changes and lossy compression), watermarks in audio from Lyria and NotebookLM, and text watermarking of Gemini app output. The public SynthID Detector portal checks for watermarks from Google and partners including OpenAI, NVIDIA and Kakao. Assume Google-generated creative is detectable as AI and disclose it where platforms or law require.** (2026-11-03; primary, high) [source](https://deepmind.google/models/synthid/) `K-59bdcac37c`
- **OpenAI plugin guidelines: plugins must not serve advertisements, must not add ChatGPT-specific fees or surcharges, and must not offer a worse version than your own site.** (2026-11-03; official, medium) [source](https://developers.openai.com/plugins/plugin-guidelines) `K-7196699033`
- **OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2025: LLM01 Prompt Injection, LLM02 Sensitive Information Disclosure, LLM03 Supply Chain, LLM04 Data and Model Poisoning, LLM05 Improper Output Handling, LLM06 Excessive Agency, LLM07 System Prompt Leakage, LLM08 Vector and Embedding Weaknesses, LLM09 Misinformation, LLM10 Unbounded Consumption. Use it as the checklist for every agent that reads web pages, emails or reviews, holds API keys, or can publish or spend.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://genai.owasp.org/llm-top-10/) `K-729b730f96`
- **Meta labels ads 'AI info' when they are fully created or materially edited with Meta's generative AI creative features; minor edits like resizing or color correction get no label. The label usually sits in the ad's three-dot menu, but when the image includes an AI-generated photorealistic human it appears next to 'Sponsored' at the top of the ad. Meta is rolling this out gradually. Expect visible labels on ads with AI people and test whether they change CTR before scaling AI-human creative.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://www.meta.com/help/artificial-intelligence/355108217670024/) `K-95ed32a11a`
- **The Universal Commerce Protocol (ucp.dev) is an open standard for agents, platforms and businesses, with specification, SDKs and reference implementations on GitHub under the Apache 2.0 license. Co-developers listed include Google, Shopify, Amazon, Walmart, Stripe, Booking.com and Marriott; Visa, Mastercard and PayPal are listed as endorsers. Capabilities are catalog search and lookup, cart building, identity linking (OAuth 2.0), checkout and order management; it runs over REST and JSON-RPC with support for AP2, A2A and MCP. Businesses remain merchant of record. A lodging spec is in draft and food ordering is coming soon.** (2026-11-03; official, medium) [source](https://ucp.dev/) `K-a1354620ad`
- **Anthropic's Usage Policy version effective 12 Nov 2026 prohibits impersonating real people or entities or creating fake personas, and lists fake reviews, sockpuppets and astroturfing as deceptive artificial activity; clearly disclosed parody, satire or fiction is allowed.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/aup) `K-ac62345525`
- **OpenAI plugin data and audience rules: collect minimum data; do not request full conversation history or precise location; protected health information is restricted. Plugins must suit general audiences including users aged 13-17 and must not target children under 13; submissions come from verified individuals or organizations, and authenticated plugins must supply a full-featured demo login.** (2026-11-03; official, medium) [source](https://developers.openai.com/plugins/plugin-guidelines) `K-c8095c8a53`

## From other topics that affect this department

- **Organization-level return policy markup: nest MerchantReturnPolicy under Organization or OnlineStore via hasMerchantReturnPolicy on one page (e.g. the returns page), not every page. Required: either applicableCountry (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2, up to 50) plus returnPolicyCategory (with merchantReturnDays for a finite window), or merchantReturnLink. Recommended: returnFees, returnMethod, refundType, restockingFee, and returnPolicySeasonalOverride for holiday windows.** (2026-11-03; official, medium) [source](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/return-policy) `K-0410b3d507`
- **Merchant listing vs product snippet markup: merchant listings require an Offer (product snippets also accept AggregateOffer) because the merchant must be the seller, and they require a price greater than zero. Use product snippet markup for editorial review or comparison pages, and merchant listing markup only on pages where shoppers can buy from you.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/merchant-listing) `K-0a5b0b5299`
- **Google Merchant Center required product attributes: id (max 50 chars), title (max 150) or structured_title, description (max 5,000) or structured_description, link on the verified domain, image_link, availability (in_stock, out_of_stock, preorder, backorder; availability_date required for preorder), price with ISO 4217 currency, brand for new products (except movies, books, music), mpn only when no GTIN, and condition for used or refurbished items. Apparel and variants also need item_group_id, color, size, gender and age_group in some countries.** (2026-11-03; official, medium) [source](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/7052112?hl=en) `K-2cb2187f7e`
- **Google merchant listing structured data (pages where the product can be bought) requires Product name, image and offers, with price (or priceSpecification.price) and priceCurrency; if shippingDetails is used it needs deliveryTime, shippingDestination and shippingRate. Recommended: availability, hasMerchantReturnPolicy, itemCondition, shippingDetails, gtin, brand.name, sku, aggregateRating and review. Add these to every product page template, not only the feed.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/merchant-listing) `K-970b4e5749`
