## Privacy, consent and data law

- **Connecticut Data Privacy Act (AG guidance): it applies to controllers processing data of 35,000+ consumers, to anyone processing sensitive data or selling personal data, and to all consumer health data controllers. Since 1 Jan 2025, every covered controller must honor opt-out preference signals such as Global Privacy Control for targeted ads and sales. Sensitive data needs consent. Selling data of, or targeting ads to, consumers under 16 needs opt-in consent, and online services may not target ads to known minors under 18. Profiling needs an impact assessment.** (2026-11-03; official, medium) [source](https://portal.ct.gov/ag/sections/privacy/the-connecticut-data-privacy-act) `K-db9a4b5319`

## AI and consumer-protection rules

- **EU AI Act timeline: in force 1 Aug 2024. Prohibitions and AI literacy have applied since 2 Feb 2025, and general-purpose AI model obligations since 2 Aug 2025. Most of the Act, including the transparency rules, has applied since 2 Aug 2026. A ninth prohibition (non-consensual sexual content and CSAM generation) applies from 2 Dec 2026. The AI Omnibus (in force 27 Jul 2026) moved high-risk Annex III rules to 2 Dec 2027 and Annex I product rules to 2 Aug 2028.** (2026-07-27; official, high) [source](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai) `K-ac0b151d96`
- **The Digital Omnibus on AI amended AI Act Article 4 (paragraphs 1-3; the Service Desk page reflects the EUR-Lex consolidated version of 27 Jul 2026): providers and deployers must take measures to support AI literacy of staff and others operating AI systems on their behalf, considering their knowledge, experience and context of use, but the obligation does not require guaranteeing any specific level of AI literacy of any individual. Keep a short record of AI training and usage guidance for everyone (including agencies and freelancers) who runs AI tools for the brand.** (2026-07-27; official, high) [source](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-4) `K-b8276d6754`
- **AI Act Article 50 has applied since 2 Aug 2026. Deployers must clearly disclose deepfakes at first exposure. AI-generated text published to inform the public on matters of public interest must be labelled unless it had substantive human review and someone holds editorial responsibility (spell-checking doesn't count). The provider's machine-readable marks don't satisfy the deployer's duty. Providers of systems already on the market before 2 Aug 2026 have until 2 Dec 2026 for the Article 50(2) marking duty. Content made before 2 Aug 2026 doesn't need retroactive labels.** (2026-07-24; official, high) [source](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/transparency-obligations-under-article-50-ai-act) `K-32ab615321`
