## AI agents, models and agentic commerce

- **OWASP LLM01:2025 Prompt Injection: direct injection comes from user input; indirect injection comes from external content (websites, files) that the model processes. OWASP says no foolproof prevention is known, only layered mitigation: constrain the model's role, validate output formats with deterministic code, filter inputs and outputs, enforce least privilege, require human approval for high-risk actions, segregate and mark untrusted external content, and run regular adversarial testing.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm01-prompt-injection/) `K-0a3bd69f71`
- **YouTube requires creators to set 'AI use' at upload for realistic AI-generated or meaningfully altered content: a real person saying or doing what they didn't, altered footage of real events or places, realistic scenes that never happened, or AI music as the main focus. Not needed for clearly unrealistic or animated content, minor edits, or AI used only for scripts, titles, thumbnails or ideas. YouTube may auto-label via its own tools or C2PA metadata. Disclosure alone does not limit reach or monetization; repeated non-disclosure can mean removal or YPP suspension.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en) `K-43608d5348`
- **Anthropic's Usage Policy effective 12 Nov 2026: consumer-facing chatbots and agents must disclose that users are interacting with AI rather than a human, at the start of each session or in the interface; political content must not be false or misleading, and mass automated messages to voters must not conceal their artificial origin; high-risk AI recommendations (legal, medical, finance, credit, employment, housing) need meaningful review by a qualified person.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/aup) `K-558ee2c6d4`
- **Google SynthID embeds invisible watermarks in AI images and video (designed to survive cropping, filters, frame-rate changes and lossy compression), watermarks in audio from Lyria and NotebookLM, and text watermarking of Gemini app output. The public SynthID Detector portal checks for watermarks from Google and partners including OpenAI, NVIDIA and Kakao. Assume Google-generated creative is detectable as AI and disclose it where platforms or law require.** (2026-11-03; primary, high) [source](https://deepmind.google/models/synthid/) `K-59bdcac37c`
- **OpenAI plugin guidelines: plugins must not serve advertisements, must not add ChatGPT-specific fees or surcharges, and must not offer a worse version than your own site.** (2026-11-03; official, medium) [source](https://developers.openai.com/plugins/plugin-guidelines) `K-7196699033`
- **OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2025: LLM01 Prompt Injection, LLM02 Sensitive Information Disclosure, LLM03 Supply Chain, LLM04 Data and Model Poisoning, LLM05 Improper Output Handling, LLM06 Excessive Agency, LLM07 System Prompt Leakage, LLM08 Vector and Embedding Weaknesses, LLM09 Misinformation, LLM10 Unbounded Consumption. - **Meta labels ads 'AI info' when they are fully created or materially edited with Meta's generative AI creative features; minor edits like resizing or color correction get no label. - **The Universal Commerce Protocol (ucp.dev) is an open standard for agents, platforms and businesses, with specification, SDKs and reference implementations on GitHub under the Apache 2.0 license. Co-developers listed include Google, Shopify, Amazon, Walmart, Stripe, Booking.com and Marriott; Visa, Mastercard and PayPal are listed as endorsers. Capabilities are catalog search and lookup, cart building, identity linking (OAuth 2.0), checkout and order management; it runs over REST and JSON-RPC with support for AP2, A2A and MCP. Businesses remain merchant of record. A lodging spec is in draft and food ordering is coming soon.** (2026-11-03; official, medium) [source](https://ucp.dev/) `K-a1354620ad`

- **OpenAI plugin data and audience rules: collect minimum data; do not request full conversation history or precise location; protected health information is restricted. - **OpenAI's Agentic Commerce Protocol docs: 'OpenAI is not the merchant of record in the Agentic Commerce Protocol' - merchants bring their own PSP; for each one-time delegated payment request OpenAI sets a maximum chargeable amount and an expiry, the PSP returns a payment token and OpenAI passes it to the merchant (Stripe's Shared Payment Token is the first compatible implementation). - **OpenAI's ChatGPT plugin guidelines (the app directory became the Plugin directory on 9 Jul 2026, per OpenAI's help center): plugins may currently sell only physical goods; digital products or services (subscriptions, digital content, tokens, credits) are not allowed, directly or indirectly (e.g. freemium upsells). Plugins may link to an informational plans page but not directly to checkout; purchases happen on your own domain. Instant Checkout is in beta for select marketplace partners only.** (2026-11-03; official, medium) [source](https://developers.openai.com/plugins/plugin-guidelines) `K-fcd05a72ee`
