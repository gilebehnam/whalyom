# Whalyom — Your Entire Marketing Department. Zero Payroll.

> Free, open-source edition (MIT). Rules: `rules/`. Commands: `docs/COMMANDS.md`. Numbers: `docs/STATS.json`.

**Whalyom** (Whalya Online and Digital Marketing Automation) is this project, an autonomous AI marketing, branding and revenue engine. Internal ids keep the earlier `om` prefix (`om-*` agents, the `om` skill); `/whalyom` is the main command. Read `docs/ARCHITECTURE_STATE.md` (what exists, how the brief maps to the code, what is open) and `docs/BRAND_IDENTITY.md` before larger changes.

**Continuity rule:** never delete or replace working parts; extend them. Every change to `tools/` keeps `npm test` green.

**Six AI layers** (field `layer` on every agent): 1 sovereign command, 2 perception & intelligence, 3 strategy/offer/brand, 4 execution & production, 5 sales/CRO/revenue, 6 self-evolution. **28 departments** (field `dept`): sovereign, intel, strategy, funnel, creative, seo, social, paid, leadgen, closer, lifecycle, finops, evolve, and since wave 9 trust, consulting, data, pr, partners, global, commerce, growth-free, ailab, and since wave 10 pmm, studio, success, martech, local, community; each has a mega head (`om-mega-<dept>`) and a knowledge brief. **AI transitions:** every handoff is recorded by `apply-results.mjs` in `transitions.json` (fired → completed, or failed → healing → healed), gated by `autonomy.transition_gate`. **Dual mode:** `budget.mode` free / paid / hybrid; budgets ≤100 run in sniper mode on one channel; `tools/killswitch.mjs` pauses variants that waste spend.

This folder is a marketing and sales operating system that runs **inside Claude Code**. Claude Code is the runtime, the `om-*` subagents in `.claude/agents/` are the workforce, and the online dashboard on claude.ai shows what they are doing.

## How it runs

- **Entry points:** `/om-mega` runs the whole pipeline end to end. The `om` skill (`.claude/skills/om/SKILL.md`) has the individual commands: `new`, `onboard`, `plan`, `auto`, `auto-all`, `run`, `landing`, `status`, `approve`, `learn`, `doctor`, `upgrade`, `schedule`, `flow`, `sync`, and the module commands `crm`, `sequences`, `inbox`, `score`, `ab`, `attribution`, `ai-visibility`, `watch`, `voice`, `chat`, `integrations`, `creators`, `reallocate`, `social`, `realtime`, `agency`, `creative`, `call`, `retail`, `new-agent`, `autonomy`, `routing`. First-run setup: `README.md`.
- **Workforce:** 536 agents in 27 pods and 28 departments (30 mega agents that head departments, 447 core specialists including the 51-agent AI Lab, the 18-agent Strategy Consulting Practice, the 17-agent Commerce & Growth Frontier and the 8-agent Zero-Budget Growth & Funnel pod, 59 micro agents for single fast jobs), generated from `tools/roster.mjs` + `tools/roster-extra.mjs` + `tools/roster-wave3.mjs` + `tools/roster-wave4.mjs` (the AI Lab, built in ten rounds) + `tools/roster-wave5.mjs` (the Strategy Consulting Practice: McKinsey, BCG, Bain, Big Four, HBR and HBS methods, skills `om-consulting-canon`, `om-problem-solving`, `om-exec-storyline`) + `tools/roster-wave7.mjs` (the Commerce & Growth Frontier: retail media and marketplaces, social commerce, CTV and programmatic, brand growth science, product-led growth, partner ecosystems, events, analyst relations, loyalty, green claims and messaging compliance, headed by `om-mega-commerce`; skills `om-retail-media`, `om-social-commerce`, `om-ctv-programmatic`, `om-brand-growth-science`, `om-plg-playbook`, `om-partner-ecosystems`, `om-green-claims`) + `tools/roster-wave8.mjs` (Zero-Budget Growth & Funnel, headed by `om-mega-growth-free`: funnel manager, lead router, free listings, community lead hunter, warm network, reviews/referrals/UGC, zero-cost partnerships, giveaways, stage-update micro agent; skills `om-zero-budget-growth`, `om-funnel-management`) + `tools/roster-wave10.mjs` (six more departments, each with a mega head, specialists, micro agents and its own method skill: Product Marketing `om-product-marketing`; Video & Audio Studio `om-video-studio`; Customer Success & Support `om-customer-success`; Web & MarTech Engineering `om-martech-ops`; Local & Field Marketing `om-local-marketing`; Community & Events `om-community-events`; 39 new agents, 23 existing agents moved in) + `tools/roster-wave9.mjs` (nine specialist departments: Trust, Privacy & Compliance; Strategy Consulting Practice; Data Science & Customer Intelligence; PR, Communications & Reputation; Partnerships, Affiliates & Creators; International & Localization; Commerce & Marketplaces; Zero-Budget Growth & Community; AI Engineering & Agentic Systems; 7 new mega heads, 18 new specialists, 116 existing agents moved in, keeping their old pack and color). Every agent masters its department's skill pack (`tools/skill-packs.mjs`); AI Lab agents also master the AI pack (installed AI skills plus the eight `om-ai-*` / `om-agentic-commerce` / `om-llm-visibility-lab` skills).
- **Knowledge and self-update:** `knowledge/` holds verified, dated facts from official and research feeds (`node tools/knowledge.mjs cycle`, daily in the daemon; `om-knowledge-refresh` workflow for the LLM steps). Agents read `knowledge/briefs/<dept>.md` and the generated `om-knowledge-*` skills before acting. `om-evolve` improves agents and, in its Grow phase, creates new agents (`om-grown-*`, `config/learned-agents.json`) and skills (`om-learned-*`) for gaps; `tools/evolve.mjs` ships only what `config/evolution.json` allows (owner-only file), into `config/agent-overrides.json`, never touching guards. See `docs/KNOWLEDGE.md`.
- **Orchestration:** only the main session dispatches agents (subagents cannot spawn subagents). Multi-wave runs use the saved workflows in `.claude/workflows/`: `om-onboard`, `om-autopilot` (the mega-agent cycle), `om-portfolio` (all products) and the specialist workflows listed in `docs/WORKFLOWS.md` (`/om flow <workflow> <slug>`).
- **Daemon (always on, no LLM):** `node tools/daemon.mjs` runs one deterministic cycle (per active product: metrics, funnel, lead score, inbox, sequences, apply-results, CRM proposals, creators, free engine, budget, kill switch, policy, execute, attribution, AI visibility, bandits, competitor watch once a day, learn; then knowledge, Needs You Know, build-state, live snapshot); `--loop <minutes>` repeats it. One scheduler at a time holds the lease in `state/scheduler.json`; `OM_KILL=1` makes a cycle only log. On Windows `node tools/install-hub.mjs` starts the hub at login; `npm run command-center` serves the local Command Center (http://localhost:4321) and `npm run mcp` the `whalyom-core` MCP server.
- **Funnel and zero budget:** `node tools/funnel.mjs <slug>` keeps `funnel/pipeline.json`, one row per lead with its stage (new, contacted, engaged, qualified, opportunity, won, nurture, lost), from `leads.jsonl`, `leads/`, `funnel/updates/` (agents and CRM exports write stage changes there, never the ledger) and Stripe payments matched by email; it writes `funnel/FUNNEL.md`, `funnel/next-actions.json`, alerts for leads waiting past the SLA, and `state/live-funnel.json` (Funnel tab). `node tools/free-engine.mjs <slug>` plans the free channels in hours, not money, with tracked links, and moves hours to channels that sell. The daemon runs both every cycle; `om-free-growth` is the weekly zero-budget workflow.
- **Product management (Whalyom improving itself):** `node tools/product-review.mjs` grades Whalyom from measured evidence (proof of results, speed to lead, zero-touch share, agents judged by sales, connectors, reliability, workforce use, knowledge, competitor parity) into `pm/SCORECARD.md` and a ranked `pm/BACKLOG.md`; `node tools/outcomes.mjs` credits leads and sales to the agents whose work went out (`state/agent-outcomes.json`). Both run every daemon cycle. `om-product-manager` and `om-competitive-product-analyst` run the weekly `om-product-evolution` workflow (local scheduled task "Whalyom: weekly product evolution", Fridays): measure, refresh `pm/competitors.json`, build the top items with tests, red-team the guards, ship only on a green suite. Earned autonomy (`product.json autonomy.earned`, `tools/lib/earned.mjs`): a type and channel the owner approved unchanged 10 times in a row runs on its own; price never, one rejection resets it, caps and compliance still apply.
- **CRM:** `node tools/crm.mjs <slug> [--propose]` keeps contacts and deals over the funnel ledger in `products/<slug>/crm/` (owner edits in `crm/edits/`, HubSpot and Pipedrive CSV `import` / `export`, pipeline view with weighted forecast and slipping deals in `crm/pipeline.json` and `CRM.md`); stage changes go back through `funnel/updates/`; `--propose` writes `crm-follow-up` approvals for consented contacts only; `--all` writes `state/crm.json` (counts and amounts, no personal data).
- **Sequences:** `node tools/sequences.mjs <slug> [--dry-run]` turns each due step of `products/<slug>/sequences/*.json` (trigger stage/channel/segment, steps with delay, channel, outbox template, quiet hours, caps, stop rules) into a pending `send` approval in `runs/SEQ-<date>.json`. Unknown consent never enters, SMS and WhatsApp need opt-in, per-lead caps hold across sequences, and `tools/execute.mjs` re-checks suppression and quiet hours right before each send. `validate <slug>` checks the definitions.
- **Inbox (reply handling):** `node tools/inbox.mjs <slug> [--dry-run]` reads `inbox/*.json` and `.eml` files, classifies each reply (bounce, unsubscribe, out of office, not now, referral, interested and so on), writes funnel updates, hashed `suppression.json` entries, `inbox/respond.json` and `runs/INBOX-*.json`. It never sends.
- **Lead scoring:** `node tools/lead-score.mjs <slug>` scores every funnel lead 0-100 from fit (`product.json lead_scoring` ICP rules) and behaviour into `funnel/scores.json` (hot/warm/cold, top reasons, no personal data), raises `funnel:hot:<lead>` alerts that resolve on contact, proposes om-sdr draft tasks, and calibrates against won and lost leads once there are 20 outcomes. `--all` writes `state/lead-scores.json`.
- **A/B and bandit tests:** `node tools/bandit.mjs <slug>` runs Thompson sampling with a 10% exploration floor over `experiments/bandits.json` (drafts from `experiments/bandit-drafts/`, beacons in `experiments/bandit-events.jsonl`). A test starts, and a winner rolls out, only through the `bandit:<id>:start` and `bandit:<id>:rollout` approvals. `snippet <slug>` prints the client script; `--all` writes `state/bandits.json`.
- **Attribution / MMM:** `node tools/attribution.mjs <slug> [--task <id>]` credits funnel touches five ways (first, last, linear, 40/20/40, 7-day time decay) and fits an MMM-lite (adstock, Hill saturation, non-negative least squares; "not enough data" under 12 weeks) into `reports/attribution.json` and `.md`. Budget shifts are pending approvals only.
- **AI-answer visibility:** `node tools/ai-visibility.mjs <slug>` turns the prompt panel (`research/ai-prompts.json`; `init <slug>` creates an empty one) and recorded answers (`research/ai-answers/<date>.json`; `record <slug> <file.json>`) into share of answer, citation rate and position per engine with Wilson intervals, competitor share of voice and a weekly trend (`reports/ai-visibility.*`). It measures only.
- **Competitor watch:** `node tools/competitor-watch.mjs <slug> [--task <id>]` crawls the pages in `research/competitors.json` politely (robots.txt, 1 request per second, public URLs only), keeps dated snapshots and writes `research/competitor-diff.json` and `.md` for the `om-competitor-watch` workflow.
- **Brand voice guard:** `node tools/voice-guard.mjs <slug> [--review]` checks every outbox draft against `voice.json` and `claims.md` (unbacked numbers, superlatives, testimonial-like quotes, banned or required terms, tone) into `reports/voice-guard.json`. `--review` writes om-micro-claim-check verdicts to `runs/REVIEW-voice-guard-<date>.json`, never lowering another reviewer's verdict.
- **On-site chat assistant:** `node tools/chat-agent.mjs ask <slug> "<question>"` answers only from verified claims, the profile, FAQ and help docs (BM25 with citations), hands off to a person on low confidence and on refund, complaint, legal, privacy, medical and safety topics, and keeps a lead only with explicit consent. `report <slug> [--run <task-id>]` proposes reply drafts as approvals; `state/chat.json` holds no personal data.
- **Integrations:** `node tools/integrations.mjs` lists connectors, readers, MCP servers and third-party manifests (`config/integrations/*.json`) with configured or missing key names only, into `state/integrations.json`. `--propose <name> --slug <slug> --task <id>` writes a pending `integration_setup` approval.
- **Creators:** `node tools/creators.mjs <slug>` keeps the creator roster (`creators/roster.json`; agents write `creators/updates/`), gates every status move (approved outreach, signed contract with a clear ad label, live deliverable), proposes outreach and payouts as approvals only, within the caps, and joins results from funnel codes. `--all` writes `state/creators.json`.
- **Budget reallocation:** `node tools/reallocate.mjs <slug>|--all [--dry-run]` proposes a new daily budget per live campaign (Thompson-style P(best) with a 10-20% exploration floor, at most 20% change a day, inside both caps) as pending `spend` approvals of kind `budget_update` in `runs/REALLOC-<date>.json`; `tools/connectors/budgets.mjs` (Meta, Google, TikTok, LinkedIn, Microsoft) changes the budget only after approval and re-checks the live caps.
- **Social publishing and inbox:** `tools/connectors/instagram.mjs`, `facebook.mjs` and `tiktok-posting.mjs` publish approved `post` / `reply` items (ad label enforced, one post per approval via `social/published.json`); `node tools/social-inbox.mjs <slug>|--all [--no-pull]` pulls Instagram and Facebook comments and DMs read-only, classifies them, drafts replies as `reply` approvals in `runs/SOCIAL-*.json` and moves buyers in the funnel (usernames stay in `products/<slug>/social-inbox/`).
- **Real-time feed:** `tools/cloud/realtime.mjs` gives the edge `GET /live/stream` (SSE) and `GET /live/<doc>` for the 15 live documents, owner token or tenant key, tenant rows filtered to its own products, personal data scrubbed, read-only.
- **Agency / white label:** `tools/cloud/agency.mjs` lets an agency tenant add clients (hashed `wyc_` keys, limits never above the plan), set branding and a custom domain, and queues client decisions for `tools/apply-decisions.mjs` (`export-decisions`); clients never write `approvals.json` and never activate.
- **AI creative:** `node tools/creative-gen.mjs <slug> <outbox/brief> [--dry-run]` generates an image (OpenAI, Stability) or video (Runway, Luma) from an approved brief, with a provenance sidecar, an AI label that cannot be removed, consent files for real people, and `product.json creative.monthly_cap` (required, counted apart from the ad caps).
- **Voice calls:** `tools/connectors/voice.mjs` (`twilio-voice`) places one Twilio call per approved `call` item after consent, calling-hours, do-not-call and cap checks, answers only from `claims.md`, and turns call outcomes into `funnel/updates/`; `check <slug> <lead>` explains a block without printing the number.
- **Retail media:** `tools/connectors/amazon-ads.mjs` (Sponsored Products, paused unless `activate: true`) and `tools/connectors/walmart-connect.mjs` (always paused) execute approved `spend` items, and both read spend, clicks, conversions, sales and ACoS into `metrics/inbox/`.
- **Agent builder:** `node tools/agent-builder.mjs options | validate <form.json> | propose <form.json>` turns a form into an `EVO-build-*` proposal under `state/evolution/proposals/`, checked by evolve's own guards and held until `om-red-team` passes it or the owner applies it with `node tools/evolve.mjs apply <id> --owner`.
- **Autonomy report:** `node tools/autonomy-report.mjs <slug>|--all` shows per type and channel whether it runs by level, by earned autonomy, is close (`7 of 10`) or stays manual, with the last break, in `reports/autonomy.md`, alerts when that changes and `state/live-autonomy.json`; it decides nothing (`tools/policy.mjs` does).
- **Agent routing:** `node tools/routing.mjs [<family|task text> [channel]]` ranks agents per work family and channel from leads, sales and evaluator scores into `state/agent-routing.json` (advisory only, never reassigns a task) with an improve-or-retire list for `om-evolve`.
- **Wiring of the 2026-11-04 modules above:** the daemon runs social-inbox, reallocate, autonomy-report and routing; the registry holds the budget, social, voice and retail connectors (budget updates routed first); the edge serves `/live/*`, `/agency`, `/client` and `/voice/*`. What is still open (creative connector, executor and apply-results hooks, retail readers) is listed per row in `docs/ARCHITECTURE_STATE.md`; until then run that tool by hand.
- **Mobile app (PWA):** the local Command Center is installable: `live/manifest.webmanifest`, `live/sw.js` (offline shell; never caches `/api` or `/stream`; local "decision waiting" notifications) and icons from `node tools/pwa.mjs`; `--check` verifies them.
- **Add a product in one command:** `node tools/add-product.mjs <domain> --cpanel https://host:2083 --cpanel-user U` (token in .env as `OM_CPANEL_TOKEN_<SLUG>`) or `--ssh user@host --ssh-key PATH`, plus `--approve-install`: workspace, instant audit, access check, WordPress plugin and must-use loader installed by `tools/connectors/site-install.mjs` (adds files only), and the hub runs the AI onboarding on Claude Platform. AI usage per month, product and workflow (real cost from each run): Command Center Growth tab and `node tools/ai-modes.mjs usage`.
- **Operating modes and AI cost:** three modes in `config/ai-modes.json` (`node tools/ai-modes.mjs plan | set <economy|balanced|aggressive> | status`). Each sets the models (`config/ai-cost.json`, written into the agent files), reviewers per draft (economy and the others review each run in one batched pass), which leads get an AI reply (hot / hot and warm / all; the rest get template sequences), the workflows the daemon schedules and the hub runs, and a hard monthly AI budget (`state/ai-spend.jsonl`). All headless AI work runs on Claude Platform through `WHALYOM_ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` (`tools/lib/claude-run.mjs` refuses to start without it; `OM_ALLOW_SUBSCRIPTION=1` is the only opt-out). The local Claude-app scheduled tasks are paused.
- **Single writer:** agents never edit `tasks.json`, `approvals.json` or `experiments.json`. Each writes `runs/<task-id>.json`; `node tools/apply-results.mjs <slug>` merges them.
- **Schedules:** recurring runs use Claude Code routines (`/schedule`) or local scheduled tasks. Each run is `/om run <product>` followed by `/om sync`.
- **Install and go:** `node tools/install-snippet.mjs` prints the one-line snippet for any site; the edge (`tools/cloud/edge.mjs`, started by the hub when `OM_EDGE_PORT` is set) onboards a new domain on its first visit (`tools/instant-onboard.mjs`: instant audit, quick wins, booster drafts that go live with one approval). Cloud hosting: `deploy/CLOUD.md`.
- **Commands, rules, numbers and the free edition:** `node tools/commands.mjs` writes the 38 ready-made slash commands (`.claude/commands/`, catalog in the script, list in `docs/COMMANDS.md`); `node tools/rules.mjs` writes the 26 house rules (`rules/`, each naming the code that enforces it); `node tools/stats.mjs` counts agents, departments, skills, commands, rules and knowledge into `docs/STATS.json` (never type these numbers by hand); `npm run oss` builds the free MIT edition into `dist/whalyom-oss/` (everything except the paid online app, pricing, licensed fonts, accounts, state and real products; texts in `tools/oss/`, leak scan with `--strict`).
- **Dashboard:** `/om sync` builds the dashboard datasets from `state/`; publish them as a claude.ai artifact of your own.

## Layout

```
products/<slug>/        one workspace per product (copy of products/_template)
  product.json          name, domain, stage, market, goals, budget caps, channels
  profile.md            product, ICP, positioning, voice
  claims.md             the only claims agents may make, each with its proof
  plan.md               current 30-day plan (written by om-cmo)
  tasks.json            task queue: id, agent, status, depends_on, output
  experiments.json      hypotheses, metric, result
  approvals.json        actions waiting for the owner
  outbox/               drafts ready to publish (posts, emails, ads, pages)
  research/             crawl, competitors, keywords, communities, VoC
  content/              articles and landing pages
  reports/              daily and weekly reports
  LEARNINGS.md          what worked and what didn't, read before every plan
playbooks/              shared learnings across products (om-learner writes)
state/                  dashboard datasets (om-reporter writes, /om sync uploads)
tools/                  roster.mjs (agent definitions) and build scripts
```

## Rules every agent follows

1. **Read before acting:** `product.json`, `profile.md`, `claims.md`, `LEARNINGS.md`.
2. **Claims:** use only claims listed in `claims.md`. No invented numbers, testimonials, reviews, or customer names.
3. **Nothing outbound without approval.** Agents write drafts to `outbox/` and an entry to `approvals.json`. Publishing, sending email, or changing ad spend happens only after the owner approves (dashboard comment or `/om approve`).
4. **Spend:** never exceed `budget.monthly_cap` or `budget.daily_cap` in `product.json`. Agents can propose a change; only the owner can raise a cap.
5. **Platform rules:** no fake accounts, vote manipulation, bulk unsolicited DMs, or scraping that breaks a site's terms. Follow CAN-SPAM and GDPR for email.
6. **Write results to files** and to `runs/<task-id>.json` (never to the ledgers), then return a short summary (what was done, files written, next suggested task).
7. **Every action is an experiment:** name the hypothesis and the metric in `experiments.json` when the action can be measured.

## Editing agents

Agents are generated from `tools/roster.mjs` and `tools/roster-extra.mjs`. Edit the roster, run `node tools/check-skills.mjs` (every referenced skill must exist), then `node tools/build-agents.mjs`. Do not edit `.claude/agents/om-*.md` by hand.

Products without a domain work from `brief.md`; add the domain to `product.json` later and run `/om onboard` again for the site-based research.
