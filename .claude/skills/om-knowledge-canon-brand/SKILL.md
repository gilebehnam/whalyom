## McKinsey

- **McKinsey, Experience-led growth: a new way to create value (2023): its exhibit shows customer-experience leaders grew revenue at more than twice the rate of CX laggards between 2016 and 2021. The article is a consulting analysis, so treat it as an association, not proof that CX causes growth. Use: treat CX as a growth lever in the plan, but benchmark against your own churn and repeat-purchase data rather than quoting the 2x as a promise.** (2023-03-23; primary, medium) [source](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/growth-marketing-and-sales/our-insights/experience-led-growth-a-new-way-to-create-value) `K-d9b7e482e9`
- **McKinsey and NielsenIQ (Feb 2023): analysis of five years of US retail sales data to June 2022, covering about 600,000 SKUs and roughly $400 billion in annual retail revenue, found products with ESG-related claims averaged 28% cumulative growth versus 20% for products without claims (28% and 20% as reported by a secondary summary). The authors say the link is correlation, not causation, and they did not verify the claims. Use: cite as correlation only, never as proof a claim lifts sales; keep any sustainability claim provable.** (2023-02-06; primary, medium) [source](https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/consumer-packaged-goods/our-insights/consumers-care-about-sustainability-and-back-it-up-with-their-wallets) `K-edea699562`
- **McKinsey 'A better way to build a brand: The community flywheel' (2022): brands that get the flywheel spinning show more than 75 percent of their brand content coming from users, and secondary reports cite an online conversion rate above 4 percent. McKinsey credits Gymshark and Drunk Elephant with pioneering the approach and describes Traeger's community of over a million people. Use: start with one agile team working from community ideas.** (2022-09-28; primary, medium) [source](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/growth-marketing-and-sales/our-insights/a-better-way-to-build-a-brand-the-community-flywheel) `K-413ed1ac33`
- **McKinsey Quarterly, The CEO guide to customer experience (August 2016): argues that end-to-end customer journeys track business outcomes more closely than individual touchpoints do, since touchpoints can score well while the whole experience disappoints. Use: map and measure the two or three journeys that matter (for example sign-up to first value) and give each one an owner, rather than scoring every touchpoint.** (2016-08-01; primary, medium) [source](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/operations/our-insights/the-CEO-guide-to-customer-experience) `K-2d54700888`

- **BCG 'Mindshare Matters. Now Marketers Can Measure It.' (May 30, 2024): brand strength tracks how tightly customers link their needs to a brand. Mindshare is costly to build and cheaper to maintain (an S-curve), is best targeted by demand space (a need, not a segment), and counts direct, indirect (word of mouth, usage) and paid exposure. BCG's First Fast Response (FFR) metric tracks it in near real time. Sample and method not stated. Use: plan brand spend by need-based demand space and track a leading consideration metric.** (2024-05-30; primary, high) [source](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2024/measuring-mindshare-for-modern-marketing-success) `K-fcead5ae2a`
