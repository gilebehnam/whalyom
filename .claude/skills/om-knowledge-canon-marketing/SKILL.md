BCG says it has consistently seen three times the ROI and ten times the speed across retail, travel, banking and healthcare-services clients (sample not stated). Start with one customer behavior as the pilot.** (2026-10-20; primary, high) [source](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2026/composable-shelf-agentic-marketing) `K-f500e871e5`
