- **Connecticut Data Privacy Act (AG guidance): it applies to controllers processing data of 35,000+ consumers, to anyone processing sensitive data or selling personal data, and to all consumer health data controllers. Since 1 Jan 2025, every covered controller must honor opt-out preference signals such as Global Privacy Control for targeted ads and sales. Sensitive data needs consent. Selling data of, or targeting ads to, consumers under 16 needs opt-in consent, and online services may not target ads to known minors under 18. Profiling needs an impact assessment.** (2026-11-03; official, medium) [source](https://portal.ct.gov/ag/sections/privacy/the-connecticut-data-privacy-act) `K-db9a4b5319`
- **UK Data (Use and Access) Act 2025: the ICO says that as of 19 Jun 2026 all of the Act's data protection provisions are in force. Some cookies, such as those used for statistics or basic website functionality, can be set without consent. Charities get an email soft opt-in for people who engaged with their work. The Act's clearer drafting also confirms that direct marketing can be a legitimate interest. The ICO's PECR guidance is flagged as under review, so check before dropping UK cookie consent for analytics tags.** (2026-06-19; official, high) [source](https://ico.org.uk/about-the-ico/what-we-do/legislation-we-cover/data-use-and-access-act-2025/the-data-use-and-access-act-2025-what-does-it-mean-for-organisations/) `K-6113e00b18`
- **Under the CCPA, businesses must honor opt-out preference signals such as Global Privacy Control as a valid request to opt out of sale or sharing. California's Delete Request and Opt-out Platform (DROP) for data brokers is live, and data brokers must register by 31 January each year.** (2026-01-01; official, high) [source](https://cppa.ca.gov/faq.html) `K-9aa6caa3a6`
- **Indiana's Consumer Data Protection Act has applied since 1 Jan 2026, enforced by the Attorney General. It covers businesses that operate in Indiana or target its residents and that process data of 100,000+ Indiana residents, or of 25,000+ residents with more than 50% of gross revenue from selling personal data. Consumers can access, correct, delete and port their data and opt out of targeted advertising, sale and profiling. Controllers must answer requests within 45 days, with extensions allowed.** (2025-12-15; official, medium) [source](https://www.in.gov/attorneygeneral/files/Indiana-Consumer-Data-Protection_Consumer-Bill-of-Rights_CDPCBOR.pdf) `K-5c7afdd89f`
- **After the Commission's April 2025 Digital Markets Act non-compliance decision on 'consent or pay', Meta committed (8 Dec 2025) to give EU users a choice between fully personalised ads and a less personalised ads option using less personal data. The option was presented to EU users in January 2026. Expect lower ad personalization and smaller addressable audiences for EU Meta campaigns.** (2025-12-08; official, high) [source](https://digital-markets-act.ec.europa.eu/meta-commits-give-eu-users-choice-personalised-ads-under-digital-markets-act-2025-12-08_en) `K-aeda49e8e3`
- **India notified the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules on 14 Nov 2025 with an 18-month phased compliance period, so most obligations bind from about May 2027. The rules cover consent notices, verifiable parental consent for children's data, breach reporting and registered Consent Managers. Maximum penalties under the Act reach Rs 250 crore for failing reasonable security safeguards and Rs 200 crore for breach-notice or child-data failures, with lower tiers for other violations (Rs 50 crore for general non-compliance).** (2025-11-17; official, medium) [source](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251117695301.pdf) `K-ed426ebd94`
- **On 17 Oct 2025 Google said Chrome will keep its current approach of offering users a choice on third-party cookies (there will be no deprecation). It is retiring most Privacy Sandbox technologies: Attribution Reporting API, Protected Audience, Topics, Private Aggregation and Shared Storage, IP Protection, Related Website Sets and others. CHIPS, FedCM and Private State Tokens remain supported. Don't plan measurement around Topics or Protected Audience.** (2025-10-17; official, high) [source](https://privacysandbox.google.com/blog/update-on-plans-for-privacy-sandbox-technologies) `K-bce3e1dabf`
- **California's CPPA regulations on CCPA updates, risk assessments, cybersecurity audits and automated decisionmaking technology (ADMT) took effect 1 Jan 2026. Risk assessment duties started 1 Jan 2026, with attestations due 1 Apr 2028. ADMT rules for significant decisions apply from 1 Jan 2027. Cybersecurity audit certifications are due 1 Apr 2028, 2029 or 2030 depending on revenue (>$100M, $50-100M, <$50M).** (2025-09-23; official, high) [source](https://cppa.ca.gov/announcements/2025/20250923.html) `K-44e66d0a36`
- **The EU Data Act (Regulation (EU) 2023/2854) has applied since 12 Sep 2025, says the European Commission. Users of connected products can access the data they generate and share it with third parties such as repair providers. The Act prohibits unfair contract terms that could prevent data-sharing, and customers can move data and switch between cloud providers. Products, apps or SaaS tied to devices need data-access flows and contract terms that comply.** (2025-09-12; official, high) [source](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/data-act) `K-56b6f24d97`
- **The Tennessee Information Protection Act took effect 1 Jul 2025. It applies to businesses with over $25M annual revenue that also process data of 175,000+ Tennessee consumers a year, or of 25,000+ with 50% of revenue from selling data. Consumers can opt out of sale, targeted advertising and profiling, and requests get an answer within 45 days. Before acting, the AG must give 60 days to cure. A written privacy program that reasonably conforms to the NIST Privacy Framework is an affirmative defense.** (2025-05-01; official, medium) [source](https://www.tn.gov/attorneygeneral/news/2025/4/30/pr25-25.html) `K-a79fbe6c2a`
- **Global Privacy Control (GPC) is a proposed specification, delivered by a browser setting or extension, that lets users signal their privacy preferences to sites. Its site frames it as a Do Not Sell or Share request under the CCPA and as a general request under GDPR Articles 7 and 21. GPC became an official work item of the W3C Privacy Working Group in Nov 2024 and is still being standardised. Treat a detected GPC signal as an opt-out of sale/sharing and switch off those tags for that visitor.** (2024-11-01; official, high) [source](https://globalprivacycontrol.org/) `K-51ee844acd`
- **Brazil LGPD transfers: ANPD Resolution CD/ANPD 19/2024 (published 23 Aug 2024) regulates international data transfers. Exporters relying on contracts had up to 12 months, to about 23 Aug 2025, to add ANPD's standard contractual clauses to their contracts. The resolution also sets procedures for specific clauses, global corporate rules and adequacy decisions. Any marketing tool or processor that sends Brazilian personal data abroad, such as US-hosted CRM, email or ads platforms, now needs ANPD's clauses in its DPA.** (2024-08-23; official, high) [source](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/resolucao-normatiza-transferencia-internacional-de-dados) `K-e87643209a`
- **Maryland Online Data Privacy Act (Ch. 455, 2024) took effect 1 Oct 2025 (one section applies only to processing from 1 Apr 2026). It covers firms handling data of 35,000+ Maryland consumers, or 10,000+ with over 20% of revenue from selling data. Sensitive data may be collected or processed only when strictly necessary for a product the consumer requested, and its sale is banned. Targeted ads to, and data sales about, consumers the controller knows or should know are under 18 are banned. A 60-day cure period runs to 1 Apr 2027.** (2024-05-09; official, medium) [source](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/2024RS/Chapters_noln/CH_455_sb0541e.pdf) `K-8dcb7a214d`
- **The Kentucky Consumer Data Protection Act (HB 15, signed 4 Apr 2024) took effect 1 Jan 2026. It gives consumers rights to access, correct, delete and port their data and to opt out of targeted advertising, sale of personal data and profiling. The Attorney General has exclusive enforcement authority.** (2024-04-04; official, high) [source](https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/record/24rs/HB15.html) `K-d0be87e6c8`
- **Google consent mode has four consent types. ad_storage controls advertising cookies and analytics_storage controls analytics cookies. ad_user_data (consent to send ad-related user data to Google) and ad_personalization (consent to personalized ads) were added in November 2023 as consent mode v2. Google says it is strengthening enforcement of its EU user consent policy, so banners for EEA and UK users must set all four.** (2023-11-01; official, high) [source](https://developers.google.com/tag-platform/security/guides/consent) `K-2f67ff0722`
