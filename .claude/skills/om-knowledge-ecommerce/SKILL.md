- **Organization-level return policy markup: nest MerchantReturnPolicy under Organization or OnlineStore via hasMerchantReturnPolicy on one page (e.g. the returns page), not every page. Required: either applicableCountry (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2, up to 50) plus returnPolicyCategory (with merchantReturnDays for a finite window), or merchantReturnLink. Recommended: returnFees, returnMethod, refundType, restockingFee, and returnPolicySeasonalOverride for holiday windows.** (2026-11-03; official, medium) [source](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/return-policy) `K-0410b3d507`
- **Merchant listing vs product snippet markup: merchant listings require an Offer (product snippets also accept AggregateOffer) because the merchant must be the seller, and they require a price greater than zero. Use product snippet markup for editorial review or comparison pages, and merchant listing markup only on pages where shoppers can buy from you.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/merchant-listing) `K-0a5b0b5299`
- **Merchant Center identifiers: submit gtin whenever the manufacturer assigned one (max 14 digits, no dashes or spaces); identifier_exists defaults to yes and should be set to no only when the product truly lacks identifiers for its category. Setting it to no for a product that has valid identifiers can get the product disapproved. ISBN-10 is deprecated; convert to ISBN-13.** (2026-11-03; official, medium) [source](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/7052112?hl=en) `K-0cb278563c`
- **Google free listings show products at no cost on the Shopping tab, Search (rich results, Popular Products, Shopping Knowledge Panel), Images and Lens, YouTube (limited countries), Maps, Gemini and Business Profile; free local listings need an opt-in. They are usually on by default (Admin access needed to change them) and work alongside paid ads. Check free listings status before spending on Shopping ads for a new store.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/9199328?hl=en) `K-2853dcb89d`
- **Google Merchant Center required product attributes: id (max 50 chars), title (max 150) or structured_title, description (max 5,000) or structured_description, link on the verified domain, image_link, availability (in_stock, out_of_stock, preorder, backorder; availability_date required for preorder), price with ISO 4217 currency, brand for new products (except movies, books, music), mpn only when no GTIN, and condition for used or refurbished items. Apparel and variants also need item_group_id, color, size, gender and age_group in some countries.** (2026-11-03; official, medium) [source](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/7052112?hl=en) `K-2cb2187f7e`
- **Merchant Center energy labels: energy efficiency class attributes now apply only to products targeting Switzerland, Norway or the UK; EU energy label data must be sent through the certification attribute. Smartphones and tablets came under EU energy label rules from 20 Jun 2025, and tumble dryers were rescaled from 1 Jul 2025. Feeds for EU appliance or phone sellers must carry certification data.** (2026-11-03; official, medium) [source](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/7052112?hl=en) `K-4b7f74cba9`
- **Free listings requirements: follow Google's free listing policies, add return policy information (Merchant Center recommended), and configure shipping settings or the shipping attribute, which is mandatory in many countries including the US, UK, Canada, Australia, Germany, France, Japan and India. Keep product data consistent with the store; appearance is not guaranteed and depends on data quality.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/9199328?hl=en) `K-77d2963f32`
- **Google merchant listing structured data (pages where the product can be bought) requires Product name, image and offers, with price (or priceSpecification.price) and priceCurrency; if shippingDetails is used it needs deliveryTime, shippingDestination and shippingRate. Recommended: availability, hasMerchantReturnPolicy, itemCondition, shippingDetails, gtin, brand.name, sku, aggregateRating and review. Add these to every product page template, not only the feed.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/merchant-listing) `K-970b4e5749`
- **Merchant Center image rules: show the product accurately with no promotional text, watermarks, borders or placeholders; no upscaled images or thumbnails; JPEG, WebP, PNG, non-animated GIF, BMP or TIFF; crawlable by Googlebot-image; up to 10 additional_image_link images. AI-generated images must keep their AI metadata (e.g. IPTC DigitalSourceType). A minimum size of 500 x 500 px for all product images is enforced from 31 Jan 2027, so re-export small images now.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/7052112?hl=en) `K-b170753d49`
- **Google applies return policy sources from strongest to weakest: Content API for Shopping return settings, then Merchant Center or Search Console settings, then product-level merchant listing markup, then organization-level markup. If both markup and Search Console settings exist, Google uses only Search Console. Fix return policy errors where the strongest source lives, or markup changes will have no effect.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/return-policy) `K-e03ba2675a`
- **Merchant API (v1) is the redesign of the Content API for Shopping, split into sub-APIs such as accounts/v1, products_v1 (write via productInputs), inventories/v1 (local and regional) and reports_v1. Resources use path names like accounts/123/products/en~US~sku123; multiple primary and supplemental data sources are supported; push notifications replace polling; product and merchant reviews can be uploaded; gRPC is the default with REST supported.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://developers.google.com/merchant/api/overview) `K-ed4b96c4d8`
- **The Google Content API for Shopping reached its sunset on 18 Aug 2026. Since 1 Sep 2026, requests without an approved extension fail intermittently with HTTP 410, and full shutdown is expected in early 2027. Feeds and integrations must use the Merchant API, generally available as v1 since July 2025. Platform-managed accounts are migrated by the platform partner.** (2026-08-18; official, high) [source](https://developers.google.com/shopping-content/guides/deprecation-and-sunset) `K-98393adfbe`
- **Google Marketing Live 2026 shopping news (20 May 2026): Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP) checkout in Universal Cart and in Direct Offers and YouTube Shopping ads is 'coming soon', with UCP rolling out to Canada and Australia, then the UK. Merchant Center AI performance insights (share of voice on AI surfaces vs similar brands) roll out in AU, CA, IN, NZ and the US. Conversational attributes for product descriptions are available now globally.** (2026-05-20; official, high) [source](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/shopping/shopping-updates-google-marketing-live/) `K-33d6d82c1f`
- **Since 12 Nov 2025, Search Console 'Shipping and returns' settings are open to any site Google identifies as an online merchant, with no Merchant Center account needed, and these settings take precedence over structured data. Google added organization-level shipping policy markup so merchants can declare one site-wide policy. Product-level shipping markup still overrides it for those items.** (2025-11-12; official, high) [source](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2025/11/more-ways-to-share-shipping) `K-13c339a221`
- **EU General Product Safety Regulation (EU) 2023/988 (Commission factsheet, 1 Jul 2023): in force 12 Jun 2023 and applicable to businesses from 13 Dec 2024, covering non-food consumer products sold offline or online. All covered products must carry information on an economic operator responsible for them located in the EU, who also acts as the contact point for consumers and market surveillance authorities. Recalls require direct contact and standardised notices, with an effective, timely and cost-free remedy; businesses communicate with authorities through the EU Safety Business Gateway; online marketplaces have specific product safety requirements.** (2023-07-01; official, high) [source](https://commission.europa.eu/document/download/a281b150-19fd-44f9-bef8-c6018f9c4792_en?filename=new_general_product_safety_regulation_-_factsheet.pdf) `K-e0906ae5f8`
