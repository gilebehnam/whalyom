# Social commerce

Social commerce turns discovery into checkout without leaving the app. It rewards entertaining product content, creator affiliates and fast fulfilment. Seller rules, fees, eligible markets and categories change often: read `om-knowledge-retail-media` and `om-knowledge-social-organic` first and cite them.

**1. Eligibility and fit.** Check the platform's seller requirements, eligible markets and restricted categories for the product. Write the margin after platform fees, affiliate commission and fulfilment before applying.

**2. Catalog.** Sync products from the store or feed so price, stock and variants match everywhere. Titles and images follow the platform's listing rules; no claim beyond claims.md.

**3. Content that sells.** Short videos that show the product in use in the first seconds, answer one objection each, and tag the product. Rotate hooks (om-hook-writer) and reuse what works. Live shopping only with a host, a run sheet and real-time answers to questions.

**4. Creator affiliates.** Open the product to the platform's affiliate marketplace with a commission from unit economics; invite creators whose audience matches the ICP; send samples only with the owner's approval. Creators use the platform's paid-partnership label.

**5. Operations.** Ship within the platform's required time, keep stock accurate and answer messages fast: late shipping and cancellations cost visibility and can suspend the shop. Returns follow the platform's policy.

**6. Measurement.** Track GMV, conversion rate per video and per creator, refunds, and net margin after fees. Compare against the store's own checkout to decide where to push.

**7. Guardrails.** No fake reviews or bought engagement, no misleading before-and-after claims, no undisclosed paid content. New shops, samples, commissions and ad spend are approvals.
