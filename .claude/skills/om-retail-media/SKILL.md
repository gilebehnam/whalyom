# Retail media and marketplaces

Marketplaces are search engines with a checkout. Ranking depends on relevance, conversion, price, availability and reviews; ads buy position while organic rank catches up. Fees, ad formats and policies change often: read `om-knowledge-retail-media` and `om-knowledge-ecommerce` first and cite them.

**1. Channel fit.** Decide per marketplace: category demand, fees (referral, fulfilment, storage, ads), margin after all fees, competition, brand-control risk and the policy limits for the category. Write the unit economics before any listing.

**2. Listing.**
- Title, bullets and description in the buyer's words, with the attributes shoppers filter on.
- Every backend attribute filled; correct category and variation family.
- Images that answer questions (scale, use, contents, comparison); video where allowed.
- Brand content (A+ or equivalent) once the brand is registered.
- The same facts as claims.md, the site and the feed. No claim the marketplace restricts.

**3. AI shopping assistants.** Marketplace assistants answer questions from the listing, reviews and Q&A. Make specs explicit in text, answer common questions in the listing, and check the assistant's answers for the top buying questions each month.

**4. Ads.**
- Start with sponsored products on exact and phrase terms that already convert; harvest search terms weekly; add negatives.
- Add sponsored brands and display when there is a brand store and proven listings.
- Off-site retail media (retailer audiences on the open web) only with a clear incrementality test.
- Bid on profit: target ACOS or ROAS derived from margin after fees, and track total ACOS (ad spend ÷ all sales) for the halo on organic rank.

**5. Operations that drive rank.** Stock-outs and late shipping cost rank; plan inventory and fulfilment first. Ask for reviews only through the marketplace's own tools; never incentivise or gate them.

**6. Risk.** No review manipulation, no fake listings or variation abuse, no keyword stuffing, no price parity tricks. Every spend change and every new listing is an approval for the owner.
