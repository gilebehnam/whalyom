- **OpenAI's product feed spec for ChatGPT accepts JSONL, CSV or TSV. Discovery requires item_id, title, description, url, brand, seller_name, image_url, availability and price. is_eligible_search defaults to true, and setting it false also blocks checkout. Checkout needs a separately onboarded integration plus seller_privacy_policy and seller_tos. Ads stay off unless a feed-level default enables them. Update prices and availability when they change; sale or availability dates in the feed don't schedule anything. Spec read 3 Nov 2026.** (2026-11-03; official, medium) [source](https://developers.openai.com/commerce/specs/feed) `K-61bbefed06`
- **Applebot crawls for Spotlight, Siri and Safari search features, and its data may also help train Apple's generative AI models. Applebot-Extended doesn't crawl: it only controls how Apple uses data Applebot has already crawled, so disallow Applebot-Extended in robots.txt to opt out of training. Applebot-Extended rules don't affect search ranking. The nosnippet directive keeps content out of AI-generated answers.** (2026-11-03; official, medium) [source](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119829) `K-a84b169d05`
- **Perplexity has two agents. PerplexityBot indexes and links sites in Perplexity search, follows robots.txt, and isn't used to train foundation models. Perplexity-User makes user-requested fetches and generally ignores robots.txt. Both publish IP ranges (perplexitybot.json, perplexity-user.json) for WAF allowlisting, and changes take up to 24 hours.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://docs.perplexity.ai/guides/bots) `K-e020ffd8ee`
- **Google-Extended is a robots.txt control token, not a separate crawler. It decides whether Google-crawled content can train future Gemini models and be used for grounding in Gemini Apps and Vertex AI. It doesn't affect inclusion or ranking in Google Search. Google's crawler documentation says Google intends to support the AIPREF training-preference signal 'user-agent: __usage_ai-training' in early 2027.** (2026-10-30; official, high) [source](https://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/crawlers-fetchers/google-common-crawlers) `K-38209e3301`
- **On 30 Oct 2026 Google announced it will support a shared AI-training policy token in robots.txt (IETF draft-kelley-aipref-training-preference-signal), alongside other AI providers, in 2027. If a site uses the shared token to opt out of training and doesn't name Google-Extended explicitly, Google will treat it as disallowing Google-Extended. Sites that explicitly allow Google-Extended stay eligible for Gemini Apps grounding and clicks from it.** (2026-10-30; official, high) [source](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/10/ai-training-control) `K-489c16e22a`
- **On 28 Oct 2026 Google expanded ads in AI Mode to Australia, Canada, India, Kenya, Malaysia, New Zealand, Nigeria, Pakistan, the Philippines and Singapore. It also launched multiturn ads in the US, which can appear in AI Mode follow-up queries labeled 'Sponsored'. Brazil, Mexico and the UK are planned for Q4 2026.** (2026-10-28; official, high) [source](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/ads-in-ai-mode-global/) `K-a697ced6ad`
- **ChatGPT Ads launched in the US on 9 Feb 2026. On 21 Oct 2026 OpenAI expanded them to 26 more countries, for 89 in total, and said more than 100,000 advertisers had run campaigns. Ads show only on the Free and Go plans (Plus, Pro and Enterprise stay ad-free). They are labeled and separate from answers. OpenAI says ads don't influence answers and conversations aren't shared with advertisers. Advertisers can buy through partners or the self-serve Ads Manager.** (2026-10-21; official, high) [source](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-ads-expands-26-new-countries/) `K-505d5a29da`
- **OpenAI uses separate user agents. OAI-SearchBot puts sites in ChatGPT search, and blocking it removes them from ChatGPT search answers (navigational links can still appear). GPTBot crawls for model training. ChatGPT-User makes user-initiated fetches, to which robots.txt may not apply. OAI-AdsBot checks the safety of landing pages submitted as ChatGPT ads. robots.txt changes take about 24 hours to apply to OAI-SearchBot.** (2026-10-21; official, high) [source](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots) `K-abfbf48fb9`
- **On 13 Oct 2026 Bing added Topics Portfolio (beta) to AI Performance in Webmaster Tools, for sites with enough data. It groups the grounding queries you're cited for into topics and shows each topic's size (total citations across all sites), the number of sites cited and the query count. Detail pages list opportunity queries, where other sites are cited and yours isn't, with volumes given as ranges. Classification is AI-generated and the numbers are directional. Start with topics where you're already cited, then target the opportunity queries.** (2026-10-13; official, high) [source](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/2026/10/introducing-topics-portfolio-beta-in-ai-performance/) `K-4146cdb394`
- **On 16 Jun 2026 Bing added four preview features to AI Performance in Webmaster Tools. Intents sorts grounding queries into types such as Informational, Commercial, Navigational, Research and Local. Topics groups related queries into clusters. Citation Share is your site's citations as a percentage of all citations shown for the same grounding query. Compare overlays an earlier period on the current one. Bing says Citation Share is not a ranking or competitive scoreboard, shows no competitor domains, and can shift with model or freshness changes.** (2026-06-16; official, high) [source](https://blogs.bing.com/search/June-2026/New-AI-Visibility-Insights-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Intents-Topics-Citation-Share-Compare) `K-ffdc773345`
- **Search Console has dedicated Search generative AI performance reports covering impressions, pages, countries, devices and dates for AI Overviews, AI Mode and generative AI features in Discover. They launched for a subset of sites on 3 Jun 2026 and reached all websites worldwide on 31 Aug 2026. This data is also still included in the overall Performance report.** (2026-06-03; official, high) [source](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/06/gen-ai-performance-reports) `K-49b66fea2e`
- **Google's own guide to generative AI in Search says Google Search doesn't use llms.txt or similar 'special' files. Adding them neither helps nor harms visibility. No special schema.org markup is needed for AI features. To appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode a page must be indexed and eligible to show with a snippet. Google calls optimizing for AI search 'still SEO'. Announced 15 May 2026.** (2026-05-15; official, high) [source](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) `K-18b4a5d05c`
- **Anthropic runs three bots, and each can be blocked separately in robots.txt. ClaudeBot collects content that may be used for model training, so blocking it excludes your future content from training. Claude-SearchBot indexes content to improve Claude's search results, so blocking it can reduce your visibility there. Claude-User fetches pages when a Claude user asks a question, so blocking it stops those user-requested retrievals. Anthropic says its bots honor robots.txt and the non-standard Crawl-delay directive. Page dated 7 Apr 2026.** (2026-04-07; official, high) [source](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8896518-does-anthropic-crawl-data-from-the-web-and-how-can-site-owners-block-the-crawler) `K-b41a23866d`
- **Bing Webmaster Tools added an AI Performance report (public preview, 10 Feb 2026). It shows citations across Microsoft Copilot, AI-generated summaries in Bing and some partner integrations: total citations, average cited pages per day, grounding queries (the phrases the AI used to retrieve your content, sampled), page-level citation counts and trends. Citation counts aren't importance, ranking, placement, clicks or traffic. Use it to find indexed pages that are rarely cited and improve them, and use IndexNow so updates are picked up quickly.** (2026-02-10; official, high) [source](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/February-2026/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview) `K-fe9c91680e`
- **On 11 Jan 2026 Google launched the Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP), an open standard for agentic commerce co-developed with Shopify, Etsy, Wayfair, Target and Walmart. It works with A2A, AP2 and MCP. Its first use is native checkout on eligible Google listings in AI Mode and Gemini, paid with Google Pay (PayPal planned), starting with eligible US retailers. Retailers stay the seller of record and use their existing Merchant Center feeds. Google also announced dozens of new Merchant Center attributes for conversational shopping (product Q&A, accessories, substitutes).** (2026-01-11; official, medium) [source](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/agentic-commerce-ai-tools-protocol-retailers-platforms/) `K-fe0d8bddb1`
