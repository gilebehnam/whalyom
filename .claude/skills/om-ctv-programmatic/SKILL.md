# CTV and programmatic

CTV and programmatic buy reach at scale; most of the risk is in where the impressions actually run and whether they changed anything. Standards (ads.txt, sellers.json, app-ads.txt, viewability and invalid traffic), platform formats and minimums change: read `om-knowledge-video-ctv` first and cite it.

**1. When it fits.** Only after search and social prove the offer converts, and only with a budget large enough for the platform minimums and a measurable test. For micro budgets (sniper mode) skip it.

**2. Where to buy.**
- Streaming platforms' own ad tools and YouTube on TV for simple, brand-safe reach.
- A self-serve DSP when the plan needs several publishers, audiences or frequency control across them.
- Private marketplaces or curated deals over open exchange for quality.

**3. Supply quality.**
- Buy only from sellers listed as authorized in ads.txt / app-ads.txt and sellers.json.
- Prefer the shortest supply path to each publisher.
- Exclude made-for-advertising sites, unknown apps and children's content (see om-placement-guard lists).
- Require MRC-accredited viewability and invalid-traffic measurement; ask for log-level or placement reports.

**4. Frequency and reach.** Set a weekly frequency cap across all CTV and video; report unique reach, not impressions. Household-level targeting on CTV is probabilistic: say so in plans.

**5. Creative.** Sound-on, designed for a lean-back screen: brand in the first seconds, distinctive assets, one message, a memorable call to action (vanity URL or QR code with tracking). Captions for other placements.

**6. Measurement.** Last-click undercounts CTV. Measure with geo or audience holdouts (om-incrementality), site-visit and search lift in test regions, QR and vanity-URL response, and MMM over time. Report cost per incremental visit or sale with intervals.

**7. Guardrails.** Every campaign launches paused and inside the caps; spend changes are approvals. No targeting of sensitive categories or minors; follow the platform's political and restricted-category rules.
