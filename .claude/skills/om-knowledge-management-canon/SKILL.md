- **Seven-step problem solving (Charles Conn and Robert McLean, developed at McKinsey; book Bulletproof Problem Solving, Wiley 2019): define the problem precisely, break it into a MECE logic tree, prioritise the branches that matter, build a workplan, run the analyses, synthesise findings, then tell the story that drives action. Use it to frame any growth or marketing question as testable hypotheses before collecting data, so analysis effort goes to the branches with the biggest impact.** (2019-01-01; primary, medium) [source](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/strategy-and-corporate-finance/our-insights/how-to-master-the-seven-step-problem-solving-process) `K-9e509f1fb6`
- **Influence model for change (McKinsey; 'The psychology of change management', McKinsey Quarterly 2003, restated in 'The four building blocks of change', April 2016): shift mindsets and behaviour through four levers: role modelling, a change story that builds understanding and conviction, formal reinforcement such as incentives and processes, and skill building. A 2015 McKinsey Global Survey found successful transformations were nearly eight times likelier to use all four than just one. Use it when rolling out a new marketing operating model or AI workflow.** (2016-04-01; primary, medium) [source](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/people-and-organizational-performance/our-insights/the-four-building-blocks--of-change) `K-c868adf7b9`

McKinsey presents the index as linked to better performance, but that evidence is McKinsey's own and correlational, not independently replicated. Pick a few practices to build rather than all of them.** (2014-04-01; primary, medium) [source](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/people-and-organizational-performance/our-insights/the-hidden-value-of-organizational-health-and-how-to-capture-it) `K-7a064fd824`
- **Consumer decision journey (Court, Elzinga, Mulder and Vetvik, McKinsey Quarterly, June 2009): based on purchase decisions of almost 20,000 consumers in five industries (autos, skin care, insurance, consumer electronics, mobile telecom) on three continents, buying is a loop, not a funnel: initial consideration, active evaluation, closure, postpurchase. The consideration set can grow during evaluation, about two-thirds of touchpoints there are consumer-driven (reviews, word of mouth), and a loyalty loop can skip re-evaluation.** (2009-06-01; primary, medium) [source](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/growth-marketing-and-sales/our-insights/the-consumer-decision-journey) `K-bff81fe2f7`
- **Three horizons of growth (Baghai, Coley and White, The Alchemy of Growth, 1999; restated in McKinsey Quarterly's Enduring Ideas series): run three portfolios at the same time. H1 defends and extends the core, H2 builds emerging businesses, H3 seeds options such as pilots, research projects or minority stakes. Use it to split a marketing or growth budget and KPI set so the core does not starve future bets. Each horizon needs its own metrics and funding logic.** (2009-01-01; primary, medium) [source](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/strategy-and-corporate-finance/our-insights/enduring-ideas-the-three-horizons-of-growth) `K-e2acd58091`
- **GE-McKinsey nine-box matrix (built by McKinsey for General Electric in the early 1970s, after the BCG growth-share matrix): score each business unit, product line or segment on industry attractiveness and competitive strength, each a weighted composite of several factors, on a 3x3 grid. High-high cells get investment, the diagonal gets selective investment, low-low cells are harvested or divested. Richer than BCG's two single metrics, but scores depend on subjective weighting.** (2008-01-01; primary, medium) [source](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/strategy-and-corporate-finance/our-insights/enduring-ideas-the-ge-and-mckinsey-nine-box-matrix) `K-6d9e71cd0d`
- **McKinsey 7-S framework (Waterman, Peters and Phillips, 'Structure Is Not Organization', Business Horizons 23(3), 1980; popularised in In Search of Excellence): seven linked elements, strategy, structure and systems (hard) plus shared values, style, staff and skills (soft). Use it before reorganising a marketing or growth team: a change to one element, such as a new channel strategy, fails if skills, systems or style are not aligned with it.** (2008-01-01; primary, medium) [source](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/strategy-and-corporate-finance/our-insights/enduring-ideas-the-7-s-framework) `K-dd47567d5f`
- **Granularity of growth (Viguerie, Smit and Baghai, McKinsey Quarterly 2007; Wiley book 2008): split revenue growth into portfolio momentum (growth of the segments you sit in), M&A, and market-share gain. For large companies in 1999-2005, about 5.5 of 8.6 points of annual growth came from momentum and 3.0 from M&A, with share gains near zero. Use it to decide where to compete at segment level before arguing over share tactics; growth varies more within industries than across them.** (2007-01-01; primary, medium) [source](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/strategy-and-corporate-finance/our-insights/the-granularity-of-growth) `K-1ea875b1f4`

## BCG

- **BCG '2027 Revenue Agenda' (October 19, 2026; survey of 398 CROs, sales, commercial and RevOps leaders at large companies in 23 countries; opinion data): 90% report meaningful AI use in sales workflows but only 6% run autonomous end-to-end deployments. Reported gains are mostly operational (33% less admin, 18% better forecasts) while only 13% cite win rates, 14% conversion and 8% quota attainment as top measured impacts. 81% set AI adoption goals but only 36% have formal targets and tracking. Point sales AI at buyer-facing proof of value and track revenue metrics, not just usage.** (2026-10-19; primary, medium) [source](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2026/cros-turning-ai-into-sales-growth) `K-147654fe96`
- **BCG with Yale, 'Agentic AI Will Make the CMO's Role More Consequential' (July 2026): AI agents judge brands on observable performance more than on marketing claims, so the brand promise must match the delivered experience. In BCG's annual survey of nearly 300 CMOs (opinion data), 96% say AI is driving end-to-end marketing transformation but only about a third have moved beyond basics, and 31% report agentic execution as a reality. BCG also reports 43% of consumer journeys are research-led and AI users rank AI tools among their top five influential touchpoints (85%).** (2026-07-16; primary, high) [source](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2026/how-agentic-ai-transforms-marketing) `K-a00ce2a302`
- **BCG 'How AI Agents Will Transform B2B Sales' (October 2025; BCG Center for Customer Insight survey of 400 US sales reps and managers): about seven in ten sellers already use general-purpose AI tools for emails, call summaries and follow-ups, but more than four in five cite inaccuracy and poor data integration, forcing manual double-checks, and about three in four feel under-supported by infrequent training. Fix CRM data quality and coaching before layering agents onto a sales team.** (2025-10-14; primary, high) [source](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2025/how-ai-agents-will-transform-b2b-sales) `K-584b2e3327`
- **BCG 'The Widening AI Value Gap' (September 30, 2025; global executive research, sample size not stated on the article page): about 5% of firms are 'future-built' and capture AI value at scale, 35% are scaling and starting to see value, and 60% report hardly any material value despite real investment. Future-built firms report about five times the AI-driven revenue gains and three times the cost reductions of others. Use it as a reality check before promising AI marketing ROI: most companies are still in the no-value group.** (2025-09-30; primary, medium) [source](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2025/are-you-generating-value-from-ai-the-widening-gap) `K-1c85d0bb4b`
- **BCG Most Innovative Companies 2025, 'In Disruptive Times, the Resilient Win' (Jun 26, 2025; 18 editions over about two decades): the share of firms calling themselves innovation leaders has fallen despite innovation being a near-universal priority; mentions of digital innovation on earnings calls doubled 2005-2024, and 25 serial innovators mention it nearly four times as often as peers. BCG expects agentic AI to speed innovation and reshape teams. Use: benchmark product and marketing innovation against digital fluency, not R&D spend.** (2025-06-26; primary, high) [source](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2025/in-disruptive-times-resilient-win) `K-583a358d68`
- **BCG AI at Work 2025 (June 2025; survey of more than 10,600 leaders, managers and frontline white-collar staff in 11 countries and regions): over three quarters of leaders and managers use gen AI several times a week, but frontline regular use stalls at about 51%. Regular use is much higher where employees get at least five hours of training plus in-person coaching, yet only about a third say they were properly trained. Only 13% see agents deeply built into daily work. Budget training hours, not just licenses, for marketing and sales AI rollouts.** (2025-06-26; primary, high) [source](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2025/ai-at-work-momentum-builds-but-gaps-remain) `K-b2dae95415`
- **BCG Personalization Index (June 11, 2025; covers 200 brands across seven sectors): only about 10% of companies qualify as personalization leaders, and leaders achieve compound annual growth rates about 10% higher than laggards. This is cross-sectional, so it shows association rather than proof that personalization caused the growth. Use it to frame the upside of a personalization program, not to forecast a specific lift.** (2025-06-11; primary, high) [source](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2025/how-consumer-experience-is-changing-across-industries-in-the-age-of-ai) `K-48eab7b63b`
- **BCG annual CMO survey on gen AI (press release June 2, 2025; about 200 CMOs in Asia, Europe and North America, fielded April-May 2025; opinion data): 83% are optimistic about gen AI, up from 74% in 2023; 71% plan to spend over $10 million a year on it over the next three years, up from 57%; 60% expect revenue gains of 5% or more in focus areas; 30% name video generation as the next content focus; about a third of B2B and nearly a quarter of B2C CMOs rank AI agents as a high investment priority. Using gen AI to measure marketing ROI ranked last.** (2025-06-02; primary, high) [source](https://www.bcg.com/press/2june2025-cmo-confidence-genai-higher-than-ever) `K-c3b122cbab`
- **BCG retail personalization (November 18, 2024): retail personalization leaders show revenue growth about 10 percentage points above laggards; returns on personalized offers run as much as three times those of mass promotions, yet retailers on average put under 5% of promotional spend into personalized offers. One large convenience chain raised personalized offers from 1% to 10% of investment, generating about $250 million in incremental sales. Use it to argue for shifting promo budget from blanket discounts to targeted offers, tested with holdouts.** (2024-11-18; primary, high) [source](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2024/personalization-in-action) `K-b06126d4e0`
- **BCG 'Where's the Value in AI?' (press release October 24, 2024; survey of 1,000 CxOs and senior executives in 59 countries across ten major industries; executive opinion data): 74% of companies had not yet shown tangible value from AI, 26% had capabilities to move past proofs of concept, and 4% were at the cutting edge. About 62% of AI value came from core functions, with sales and marketing at 20%. Roughly 70% of AI challenges were people and process issues, 20% technology, 10% algorithms.** (2024-10-24; primary, high) [source](https://bcg.com/ja-jp/press/24october2024-ai-adoption-in-2024-74-of-companies-struggle-to-achieve-and-scale-value) `K-68f6bb32c8`
- **BCG personalization framework (Mark Abraham and David Edelman, book 'Personalized: Customer Strategy in the Age of AI', HBR Press): personalization is framed as five promises a company makes to customers, Empower Me, Know Me, Reach Me, Show Me and Delight Me, each combining a human touch with technology, and BCG calls it a $2 trillion opportunity. Use the five promises as an audit checklist for a personalization roadmap: data and consent, targeting, channel reach, relevant content, and moments that surprise.** (2024-10-15; primary, medium) [source](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/marketing-sales/personalized-customer-strategy-in-the-age-of-ai) `K-7e058d874f`
- **BCG Strategy Palette (Reeves, Haanaes and Sinha, Your Strategy Needs a Strategy): match the strategic approach to how predictable and malleable the environment is. Classical suits stable markets and rewards optimal positioning; Adaptive rests on serial temporary advantage; Visionary pursues a spotted opportunity single-mindedly to create or recreate an industry; Shaping influences how a market develops with other players; Renewal restores vitality in a harsh environment. Use it before a marketing plan to decide whether to plan, test or shape.** (2015-01-01; primary, medium) [source](https://www.bcg.com/publications/collections/your-strategy-needs-strategy/intro) `K-560105fe23`
- **Experience curve (Bruce Henderson, BCG, 1968): unit costs tend to fall by a steady 20-30% each time accumulated volume doubles, in constant dollars, but only when management actively drives costs down. Implication Henderson drew: share leaders gain a structural cost edge, so pricing a new product low enough to win its segment can pay off. Use it in growth work to test whether volume-led share plays and penetration pricing are justified by real scale economies, not as a law that holds automatically.** (1968-01-01; primary, high) [source](https://www.bcg.com/publications/1968/business-unit-strategy-growth-experience-curve) `K-63bde994dd`

## Bain

- **Bain Generative AI Survey (published November 24, 2025; waves from Q4 2023 to Q3 2025, the latest n=197 executives): 74% rank AI a top-three strategic priority (60% a year earlier). Marketing and customer service sit in a second tier, where roughly a fifth to a third of use cases have moved from pilot to scale (about 32% for customer service), behind software development at 40%. Of the 59% meaningfully adopting, about 80% of use cases met or exceeded expectations, but only about 23% of all respondents tied AI to revenue or cost. Opinion data. Use: expect marketing AI to be mid-pack; require a measured revenue or cost result per use case.** (2025-11-24; primary, medium) [source](https://www.bain.com/insights/executive-survey-ai-moves-from-pilots-to-production/) `K-0e1a33f551`
- **Bain on agentic AI in retail (press release November 13, 2025; Bain Consumer Lab gen AI survey, US): 30% to 45% of US consumers use gen AI for product research and comparison, but about half are not comfortable letting agents complete purchases end to end, and consumers trust retailers' own on-site agents about three times more than third-party agents. AI drives up to 25% of referral traffic for some retailers but under 1% of total traffic. Bain advises protecting first-party data and checkout and building perks agents cannot copy.** (2025-11-13; primary, high) [source](https://www.bain.com/about/media-center/press-releases/20252/agentic-ai-poised-to-disrupt-retail-even-with-50-of-consumers-cautious-of-fully-autonomous-purchasesbain--company/) `K-55418e2901`
- **Bain Technology Report 2025, AI in sales (September 23, 2025; based on Bain's work with B2B and B2C technology and consumer companies, no survey sample stated): sellers may spend only about 25% of their time actually selling, and Bain says AI could double that by absorbing non-selling work; step-change gains across the funnel can add up to more than a 30% increase in win rates, with early successes at 30% or better. Bain identifies 25 use cases across the sales life cycle and advises cleaning up old, inaccurate or confusing data and content first, sometimes as much as 80%. Treat the win-rate figure as potential, not an average result.** (2025-09-23; primary, medium) [source](https://www.bain.com/insights/ai-transforming-productivity-sales-remains-new-frontier/) `K-d89b0ec068`
- **Bain Commercial Excellence pricing (April 23, 2025; Bain Commercial Excellence survey of executives; opinion data): 55% of surveyed companies raised list prices at least as much as their input costs; 67% name competitive pressure and customer resistance as the top barrier, 39% insufficient data or analytics, 37% pricing skill gaps.
