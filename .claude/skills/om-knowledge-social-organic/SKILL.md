- **X's open-sourced For You algorithm (xai-org/x-algorithm, README last updated 26 Oct 2026) ranks posts with a transformer model, Phoenix. The score is a weighted sum of predicted action probabilities, with negative actions such as mute, block and report carrying negative weights. After scoring, repeat posts from the same author are decayed, posts from accounts the viewer doesn't follow are discounted, and a New-Author Boost lifts authors whose impressions are below a threshold. An age filter removes posts older than 48 hours.** (2026-10-26; official, high) [source](https://github.com/xai-org/x-algorithm) `K-0763a5e6ae`
