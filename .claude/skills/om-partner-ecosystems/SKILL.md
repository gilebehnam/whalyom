# Partner ecosystems

Partners sell to audiences the product cannot reach alone. Programs fail when terms are unclear, partners are not enabled, or attribution is unfair. Disclosure rules, platform branded-content tools and network policies are in `om-knowledge-partnerships`: read it first and cite it.

**1. Partner types and fit.**
- Affiliates and publishers: content and review sites, paid per sale.
- Creators: audience trust, paid per post, per sale or both, with usage rights.
- Referral partners and customers: warm introductions.
- Resellers and agencies: sell, implement or bundle; need margin and enablement.
- Technology partners: integrations and marketplace listings, co-marketing.
Pick one or two types that match how the ICP buys. Do not launch all at once.

**2. Program design.**
- Commission or margin from unit economics: the most the product can pay per sale and still hit the target CAC.
- Cookie or attribution window, what counts as a sale, refunds and clawbacks.
- Tiers that reward performance with higher rates, co-marketing or leads.
- Written terms: brand use, forbidden tactics (brand-keyword bidding, coupon poaching, spam, fake reviews), payment schedule.

**3. Recruitment.** Find partners who already serve the ICP (om-affiliate-recruiter, om-influencer-scout, om-partner-scout). Personal outreach with a reason they would care; no bulk unsolicited messages.

**4. Enablement.** Partner kit: positioning, claims they may make (from claims.md only), assets, demo or trial access, tracked links, and a named contact. Short onboarding and a monthly update.

**5. Attribution and payouts.** Tracked links or codes per partner; reconcile with payment data; check for fraud before payouts (om-affiliate-fraud). Report partner-sourced and partner-influenced revenue separately.

**6. Disclosure.** Every partner must disclose the relationship clearly and close to the claim, using the platform's branded-content tools where they exist. Check content before and after it goes live (om-endorsement-compliance). Contracts and payouts are approvals for the owner.
