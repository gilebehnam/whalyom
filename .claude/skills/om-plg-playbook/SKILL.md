# Product-led growth playbook

In product-led growth the product does most of the selling: people try it, reach value, and pay when they need more. Benchmarks and subscription rules (trial disclosures, cancellation, app-store offer rules) live in `om-knowledge-plg` and `om-knowledge-pricing`: read them first and cite the fact.

**1. Entry model.** Pick one and say why:
- Free plan: large top of funnel; needs a cheap-to-serve core and a clear paid line.
- Free trial: time-boxed full access; works when value shows within the trial.
- Reverse trial: full access, then falls back to free; keeps users who are not ready to pay.
- Paid trial or demo-led: for complex or high-touch products.

**2. Activation.** Define the aha moment as a measurable action, the time to reach it and the share of signups who do. Cut every step before it that is not needed; use templates, sample data and checklists. Track activation by signup cohort and channel.

**3. Product-qualified leads.** A PQL combines fit (company, role, use case) with usage signals (seats, key feature use, limits hit, invites). Route PQLs to sales or to a targeted upgrade message; write the rule with om-lead-scorer and test it against conversion.

**4. Upgrade moments.** Ask for payment when the user hits a limit they care about, wants a team feature, or reaches a success milestone, not on a timer alone. Show the value already gained and exactly what the paid plan adds.

**5. Pricing.** Price on a value metric that grows with the customer's success (seats, usage, outcomes). Keep plans few and the free line obvious. Test with om-price-tester before changing live prices.

**6. Virality and loops.** Build sharing into the work itself: invites, shared links with attribution, templates others can copy, "made with" marks the user can turn off. Measure the loop: invites per active user, invite acceptance, activation of invited users.

**7. Fair subscriptions.** Clear price and renewal terms before payment, reminders before a trial converts where rules or good practice require them, and cancellation as easy as sign-up. No hidden fees or dark patterns.
