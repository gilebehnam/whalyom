- **Apple custom product pages: up to 70 extra versions of the App Store page per app, each with its own screenshots, app previews and promotional text per localisation. Pages can be assigned keywords so they show in organic search for those terms (each keyword combination unique to one page), can carry a deep link (iOS/iPadOS 18+), are reviewed without an app update, and can be the destination of Apple Ads on Today, Search and results. Build one page per ad angle or audience.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/custom-product-pages/) `K-48468fdabc`
- **Apple App Review Guidelines 3.1.1(a) and 3.1.3: apps on the United States storefront may include buttons, external links or other calls to action pointing to purchase methods other than in-app purchase, and need no entitlement to do so. In every other storefront this stays prohibited outside the specific StoreKit External Purchase Link entitlements. Show web-checkout CTAs only in US builds or metadata.** (2026-11-03; official, high) [source](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) `K-797d9b52cb`
- **From 16 Nov 2026, App Store Connect's age rating questionnaire adds two content descriptors: 'Shared Location' and 'Dating and Relationships'. Dating and Relationships forces a minimum 18+ rating, and Brazil requires A16 for Shared Location and A18 for dating. Every app must complete both declarations by 12 Jan 2027, and they are required with any update before then. Announced 2 Nov 2026.** (2026-11-02; official, high) [source](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=yxkh08us) `K-a3eda4c4b3`
- **Google Play in the US (after the Ninth Circuit upheld the Epic injunction on 12 Sep 2025): since 29 Oct 2025 Google no longer stops developers from telling US users about pricing or availability outside Play, linking to downloads or transactions, or using non-Play billing, and doesn't require price parity. The external content links and alternative billing programs started 9 Dec 2025. Since 22 Jul 2026, US listings are shared with third-party US Android app stores unless the developer opted out.** (2026-10-21; official, high) [source](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/15582165?hl=en) `K-6ef6531525`
- **Google Play US alternative billing fees apply from 1 Oct 2026 (authorised US transactions must be reported within 24 hours): 10% on a developer's first $1M annual earnings; above that, 10% on auto-renewing subscriptions and, on other digital purchases, 20% for new installs (first install or first update from Google Play on or after 30 Jun 2026) or 25% for existing ones; for Play Games Level Up or Apps Experience program members, 10% on subscriptions and 15% (new installs) or 20% (existing installs) on other digital purchases.** (2026-10-21; official, high) [source](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/16497028?hl=en) `K-a715edff66`
- **Android developer verification: from 30 Sep 2026, verification protections apply to users on certified Android 7+ devices in Brazil, Indonesia, Singapore and Thailand installing apps from participating stores, so apps must come from registered, verified developers; global expansion to all apps follows from 2027. Google Play auto-registers about 99% of apps; register the rest in Play Console, or in the Android Developer Console if you distribute only outside Play. Hobbyists can share with up to 20 devices without ID; power users get an 'advanced flow' to install unverified apps.** (2026-09-30; official, high) [source](https://developer.android.com/developer-verification) `K-baf6595a35`
- **Apple SDK and OS minimums: since 28 Apr 2026, apps uploaded to App Store Connect must be built with Xcode 26 or later using an iOS/iPadOS/tvOS/visionOS/watchOS 26 SDK; since 9 Sep 2026, iOS and iPadOS apps must target iOS 13 or later. Builds from older toolchains are rejected at upload, so check the build chain before any launch date.** (2026-09-09; official, high) [source](https://developer.apple.com/news/upcoming-requirements/) `K-ae012dd344`
- **From 31 Aug 2026, new apps and updates on Google Play must target Android 16 (API level 36). Wear OS and Automotive must target API 35, and TV and XR API 34. Existing apps must target at least API 35 to stay visible to new users on newer Android versions. Developers could request an extension to 1 Nov 2026.** (2026-08-31; official, high) [source](https://developer.android.com/google/play/requirements/target-sdk) `K-fd5442409b`
- **Apple EU terms: from 1 Oct 2026 all EU developers are on one business model (Attachment 14 of the Developer Program License Agreement). Commission: Apple IAP 26% (reduced 15%); alternative in-app payment processing 20% (10%); out-of-app offers via actionable link 15% (10%), only on sales within 7 days of the tap; apps outside the App Store pay a 5% Core Technology Commission, replacing the per-install Core Technology Fee. Reduced rates cover Small Business Program members and subscriptions after year one.** (2026-08-18; official, high) [source](https://developer.apple.com/support/dma-and-apps-in-the-eu/) `K-2bc801214e`
- **Apple EU payment rules (from 1 Oct 2026): payment options offered (IAP, alternative processing, web links) must stay available for 12 months; alternative payment transactions are reported monthly within 15 days after month end; developers collect and remit tax on alternative processing. Kids category apps need a parental gate and may not link to web purchases; under-13s get a gate and no out-of-app offers; 13-17 need a gate for both.** (2026-08-18; official, high) [source](https://developer.apple.com/support/dma-and-apps-in-the-eu/) `K-4fe98419ea`
- **Apple EU alternative distribution: alternative app marketplaces and Web Distribution are enabled in App Store Connect and pay the 5% Core Technology Commission. Eligibility needs at least one of: a moderate Dun & Bradstreet financial-stability score, being publicly traded (or owned by such a company), venture funding from an established firm, an audit by a licensed accountant, an approved government, education or nonprofit fee waiver, a USD 1,000,000 stand-by letter of credit, or one million first-year installs worldwide. No EU legal entity is needed.** (2026-08-18; official, high) [source](https://developer.apple.com/support/dma-and-apps-in-the-eu/) `K-7f0d44ab74`
- **Since 4 Jun 2026, Texas law SB 2420 applies to new Apple Accounts in Texas. Downloads, in-app purchases and significant app changes by under-18s need age assurance and parent or guardian consent, and parents can revoke consent. Developers must use the Declared Age Range API, the Significant Change API (PermissionKit), StoreKit's AppStore.ageRatingCode, and App Store server notifications for consent withdrawal.** (2026-06-03; official, high) [source](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=sg176nne) `K-f00fa85f04`
- **Apple moved all App Store apps to its new age rating system (shown on iOS 26 and later). Developers had to answer the updated age rating questions in App Store Connect by 31 Jan 2026 or face interruptions submitting updates.** (2025-07-24; official, high) [source](https://developer.apple.com/news/upcoming-requirements/?id=07242025a) `K-257b2dc4ea`
- **Apple and the EU Digital Services Act: since 17 Feb 2025, apps without verified trader status in App Store Connect are removed from the EU App Store until it is provided and verified; app updates have required it since 16 Oct 2024. Set trader status with business contact details before any EU launch.** (2025-02-17; official, high) [source](https://developer.apple.com/news/upcoming-requirements/) `K-c2d38bbd53`
- **Apple privacy manifests: a privacy manifest describes the privacy practices of third-party code in one standard format, and Xcode combines the manifests of all third-party SDKs into one report to help create accurate Privacy Nutrition Labels. For SDKs on Apple's list (e.g. Firebase, FBSDKCoreKit, OneSignal, Flutter, Alamofire), a privacy manifest is required when a new app includes one or an update adds one; signatures are also required when a listed SDK is used as a binary dependency.** (2024-05-01; official, medium) [source](https://developer.apple.com/support/third-party-SDK-requirements/) `K-37d750f6cd`
