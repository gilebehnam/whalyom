Meta stresses that results still depend on the conversion goal, the quality of shared conversion data, and how much optimization advertisers hand to the models.** (2026-10-28; official, high) [source](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/three-new-ai-innovations-in-ads-ranking) `K-6243a1bc21`

Conversational campaign creation in the Meta AI business assistant is starting tests.** (2026-10-06; official, high) [source](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/advertising-week-new-york-2026) `K-9b8e727ca7`
- **TikTok's upgraded Smart+ puts manual and Smart+ setup in one flow, from full automation to fully manual. Each ad works like an asset group with its own URL, product set and up to 50 creatives, and reporting is grouped by creative material ID. It covers the Traffic, Sales, Lead Generation and App Promotion objectives, but the help page says it may not be available to every account yet (last updated October 2026).** (2026-10-01; official, high) [source](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/about-updates-to-smart-plus) `K-ef5b383ddf`
- **Meta ads in WhatsApp Status: the placement runs only with Instagram Stories also selected (standalone Status campaigns are not supported). Formats are single image, single video or carousel (up to 10 cards); collection and flexible formats are unavailable. A/B tests, Dynamic Creative, Reach and Frequency buying and Advantage+ Creative don't work there. user_age_unknown defaults to true, so creative must suit all ages. A WhatsApp identity can be set via wamo_whatsapp_identity_spec (new in v26.0).** (2026-07-29; official, high) [source](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/ads-in-whatsapp-status) `K-116d20c0e1`
- **Meta Marketing API v26.0 (29 Jul 2026) breaking changes, applied to all versions on 27 Oct 2026: poll ads (poll_spec) are no longer accepted. Web and App campaigns can't use applink_treatment=web_only (use automatic or deeplink_with_web_fallback). Delivery Estimate fields daily_outcomes_curve, budget_guardrail and estimate_dau are removed. Housing, employment and financial (HEF) ad sets with custom audience or detailed targeting must set advantage_audience explicitly or the API errors.** (2026-07-29; official, high) [source](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/marketing-api-changelog/version26.0) `K-316fcea161`
- **Threads ads via Meta's Marketing API need a Threads account that is Instagram-associated, Instagram-backed or Page-backed, and at least an Advertiser role on the Page linked to your Instagram account. Instagram-associated Threads accounts created before 29 Jan 2026 were added to the Business Portfolio automatically; newer ones must be added by hand. Only image, video and carousel formats work, ads can't be created from existing Threads posts, and Meta keeps Threads ad volume intentionally low during testing, so expect limited delivery.** (2026-07-29; official, high) [source](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/ad-creative/threads-ads) `K-4bfcb5d12f`
- **Meta Marketing API v26.0 targeting and placement changes: since 1 Sep 2026 (all versions) Lookalike country and location_spec are ignored, so lookalikes are countryless and delivery follows the ad set's locations. Messenger Stories was removed from all versions in late August 2026 and stripped from campaigns automatically. Instagram Explore Feed is no longer available from v26.0. Set geography on the ad set, not on the lookalike, and remove old placement values from manual placement specs.** (2026-07-29; official, high) [source](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/marketing-api-changelog/version26.0) `K-f3071b02f2`

Advice: launch with the defaults, A/B test one lower-funnel or catalogue-sales campaign against your current setup, then scale the winner.
