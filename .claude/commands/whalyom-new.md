argument-hint: "<slug> [domain]"

Use the `om` skill and run its `new $ARGUMENTS` command.

Without a domain, ask the owner for a five-line brief (what it is, who buys it, price, market, goal) and write `brief.md`.

Finish by suggesting `/whalyom-onboard <slug>`.

Rules: follow `rules/` (claims only from `claims.md`, nothing outbound without the owner's approval, spend inside the caps, platform and privacy rules).
