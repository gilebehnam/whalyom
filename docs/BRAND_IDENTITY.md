# Whalyom brand identity

**Whalyom** — *Whalya Online and Digital Marketing Automation*. Category: Autonomous AI Marketing, Branding & Revenue Engine.

**Primary slogan:** Your Entire Marketing Department. Zero Payroll.

| Context | Line |
|---|---|
| Autonomy | Hundreds of AI Marketing Employees. Working 24/7. Zero Human Friction. |
| Ocean | Rule the Digital Ocean Without a Crew. |
| Revenue | Drop Any Product In. Watch Revenue Multiply on Full Autopilot. |
| Dual budget | Infinite Organic Hustle at $0. Instant Algorithmic Scale at Any Budget. |

**Name:** *Whale / Whalya* for scale, depth and sonar-like intelligence across data; *-om* for automation, omni-channel and autonomous operations.

**Archetype:** Sovereign + Magician. **Mission:** give SMEs, startups and founders a self-driving marketing department so they never need to hire one. **Vision:** the operating system for autonomous digital commerce.

**Voice:** authoritative, precise, data-led, action-first. No filler, no passive voice. Bold in tone, strict on facts: every claim Whalyom makes about a client's product must be in that product's `claims.md`, and Whalyom never promises revenue it cannot measure.

**Logo (brand kit v1, 2026-11-05):** a round maze of three rings opened by a whale's tail, with one brass arc at the top right. The approved artwork is in `assets/brand/kit/` (horizontal English, horizontal Persian, symbol, stacked; PNG, transparent), with `tokens.json`, the Persian guide `README_FA.md` and the visual identity PDF. Web sizes are in `assets/brand/web/` (served at `/brand/*.png`). Rules from the kit: use the artwork as supplied and never set the wordmark as live text; the display spelling is WHA⅃YOM (only the L mirrored: stem right, foot left); never mirror the symbol or the Persian logo, and keep the brass arc where it is in both languages; clear space a quarter of the symbol's diameter. `tools/make-logo.mjs` holds a vector redraw of the symbol for the favicon, app icons and small inline marks (`--live` puts the kit logos into the Command Center masthead, `--png` renders the icons); the full symbol at 16 px is not approved by the kit.

**Palette (kit tokens):** ink `#090D10`, surface `#0C1B20`, raised `#10262C`, ivory text `#D5D3C8`, brass identity `#AD9570`, signal teal for data and focus `#8EDDE2`, action yellow `#E4BE4B` (with ink text), secondary text `#A8B8BA`. Brass belongs to identity, yellow to action, teal to data and selection. Persian type: the owner's licensed Samarqand (the kit suggests Ravi when licensed; Vazirmatn is the fallback); no letter spacing on Persian.

**Tokens:** `config/brand-tokens.json` — ocean-abyss `#050B14`, leviathan-navy `#0A192F`, electric-cyan `#00F0FF`, synapse-violet `#8B5CF6`, bioluminescent-emerald `#10B981`, solar-amber `#F59E0B`, crimson-pulse `#EF4444`, signal-pure `#F8FAFC`. The Command Center (`live/index.html`) uses them.
